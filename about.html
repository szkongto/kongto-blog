About Kongto Technology —Industrial Video Display Solutions

Company Overview

Kongto Technology Co.,LTD (Kongto Technology) was founded in 2013 in Shenzhen, China. We are a technology-driven company specializing in industrial video display solutions. With more than a decade of industry experience, we have provided CNC display upgrade services to over 500 manufacturing enterprises across China, the United States, Germany, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, India, and other countries.

Our core product lines include: FANUC A61L Series CRT-to-LCD retrofit modules, Mitsubishi MDT962B/BM09DF/FCUA-CT100 LCD replacements, Siemens SINUMERIK display LCD upgrades, Mazak CD1472/C5470/DR5614 series replacements, industrial video signal converters (CGA/EGA/RGB to VGA/HDMI), and custom industrial control displays (KTV Series 7—5 inch). All products feature plug-and-play design, retaining original connectors and mounting dimensions —no CNC system parameter modifications required.
