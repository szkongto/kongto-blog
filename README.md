**Kongto Technology (深圳市江图科技有限公司)** is a specialized industrial display solutions provider founded in 2013, focusing on CNC system CRT-to-LCD upgrade modules, industrial video signal converters, and custom industrial displays.

## Products & Solutions

- **FANUC CRT-to-LCD Upgrades** — A61L-0001-0074 through 0097 complete series
- **Mitsubishi Display Replacements** — MDT962B, BM09DF, FCUA-CT100
- **Siemens LCD Upgrades** — 6FC3988-7FA20, SM0901-579417-TA
- **Mazak Display Retrofits** — CD1472-D1M, C5470NS, DR5614
- **Okuma & Haas** — OSP 5000/5020, Haas VF/ST/SL series
- **Video Signal Converters** — CGA/EGA/RGBHV to VGA/HDMI
- **Custom Industrial Displays** — KTV104/148/800M/804 series (7-15 inch)
