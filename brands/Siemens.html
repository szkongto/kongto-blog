About Siemens CRT Display Upgrades

Siemens SINUMERIK is one of the most widely used CNC control systems in high-end machine tools. The CRT displays (6FC3988-7FA20, SM0901, 6FC5200 series) have been discontinued for years, with common failures including flyback transformer burnout, display dimming, and startup issues. OEM replacement parts are extremely expensive.

Kongto Technology offers Siemens SINUMERIK CRT-to-LCD upgrades covering 6FC3988-7FA20, 6FC5200 series, and SM0901 models. LCD modules use the original DB-25 connector and support AC 110V/220V power supply — no CNC parameter changes needed. Installation takes 15-20 minutes.

Siemens CRT Upgrade FAQ

Q: Which Siemens models are supported?
A: 6FC3988-7FA20, 6FC5200 series, SM0901 (NFP 579417 TA) for SINUMERIK 810D/840D/802D controls.
Q: Does the upgrade require CNC parameter changes?
A: No. The LCD module uses the original DB-25 connector and AC power — true plug-and-play with zero parameter modifications.

Frequently Asked Questions About Siemens CRT to LCD Upgrade

Is Siemens still making CRT displays?

Siemens discontinued CRT display production years ago. OEM replacements are no longer available. Our LCD module is the only practical solution to keep your CNC running with a clear, reliable display.

Will the LCD replacement work with my existing Siemens CNC electronics?

Yes. The LCD module uses the same connector and signal protocol as the original Siemens CRT. No changes to the CNC control, parameters, or wiring are needed. True plug-and-play replacement.

How long does installation take?

10-15 minutes for a technician. Power off, remove old CRT, disconnect cables, mount LCD module, reconnect, power on. No soldering, no special tools, no parameter changes required.

What is the image quality improvement?

Our LCD modules offer 800x600+ resolution (vs 640x400 CRT), 350-450 cd/m2 brightness, 50,000+ hour lifespan. Text is sharper, no flicker, readable in direct sunlight. Significant upgrade over the original CRT.

What warranty do you offer on Siemens LCD replacements?
