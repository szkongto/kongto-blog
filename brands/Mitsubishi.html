About Mitsubishi CRT Display Upgrades

Mitsubishi Electric is a leading Japanese CNC system manufacturer. CRT displays used in M64, E60, M500, M520 series controls (models MDT962B, BM09DF, FCUA-CT100) face common age-related failures including display blurring, color shift, and flyback transformer failure. Replacement parts are increasingly scarce and expensive.

Kongto Technology offers complete Mitsubishi CRT-to-LCD upgrades covering MDT962B-1A/4A, BM09DF, FCUA-CT100, and MDT1283B. All modules use the original 20-pin or 26-pin connector with DC 24V power supply for true plug-and-play installation taking only 10-15 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions About Mitsubishi CRT to LCD Upgrade

Is Mitsubishi still making CRT displays?

Mitsubishi discontinued CRT display production years ago. OEM replacements are no longer available. Our LCD module is the only practical solution to keep your CNC running with a clear, reliable display.

Will the LCD replacement work with my existing Mitsubishi CNC electronics?

Yes. The LCD module uses the same connector and signal protocol as the original Mitsubishi CRT. No changes to the CNC control, parameters, or wiring are needed. True plug-and-play replacement.

How long does installation take?

10-15 minutes for a technician. Power off, remove old CRT, disconnect cables, mount LCD module, reconnect, power on. No soldering, no special tools, no parameter changes required.

What is the image quality improvement?

Our LCD modules offer 800x600+ resolution (vs 640x400 CRT), 350-450 cd/m2 brightness, 50,000+ hour lifespan. Text is sharper, no flicker, readable in direct sunlight. Significant upgrade over the original CRT.
