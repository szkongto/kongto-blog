About Mazak CRT Display Upgrades

Mazak is a world-leading Japanese CNC machine tool builder. Its Mazatrol control systems (T-Plus, M-Plus, Fusion 640) use CRT displays (CD1472-D1M, C5470NS, DR5614, MDT1283B) that have been fully discontinued. OEM replacement parts are increasingly hard to find and expensive.

Kongto Technology offers Mazak CRT-to-LCD upgrades for all major models. Modules use the original 26-pin or 20-pin connectors with DC 24V or DC 12V power supply — true plug-and-play with no machine parameter changes required.

Mazak CRT Upgrade FAQ

Q: Which Mazak CRT models can be upgraded?
A: CD1472-D1M (26-pin), C5470NS (26-pin), DR5614 (26-pin), MDT1283B (20-pin/DC 12V), AIQA8DSP40 (26-pin), TR-120S9C (20-pin/DC 12V).
Q: What display improvements will I see?
A: Resolution increases from 640×400 to 800×600, brightness to 350-450cd/m², lifespan from 15,000 to 50,000+ hours, and 75% lower power consumption.

Frequently Asked Questions About MAZAK CRT to LCD Upgrade

Is MAZAK still making CRT displays?

MAZAK discontinued CRT display production years ago. OEM replacements are no longer available. Our LCD module is the only practical solution to keep your CNC running with a clear, reliable display.

Will the LCD replacement work with my existing MAZAK CNC electronics?

Yes. The LCD module uses the same connector and signal protocol as the original MAZAK CRT. No changes to the CNC control, parameters, or wiring are needed. True plug-and-play replacement.

How long does installation take?

10-15 minutes for a technician. Power off, remove old CRT, disconnect cables, mount LCD module, reconnect, power on. No soldering, no special tools, no parameter changes required.

What is the image quality improvement?

Our LCD modules offer 800x600+ resolution (vs 640x400 CRT), 350-450 cd/m2 brightness, 50,000+ hour lifespan. Text is sharper, no flicker, readable in direct sunlight. Significant upgrade over the original CRT.
