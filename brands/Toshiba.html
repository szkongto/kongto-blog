Frequently Asked Questions About Toshiba CRT to LCD Upgrade

Is Toshiba still making CRT displays?

Toshiba discontinued CRT display production years ago. OEM replacements are no longer available. Our LCD module is the only practical solution to keep your CNC running with a clear, reliable display.

Will the LCD replacement work with my existing Toshiba CNC electronics?

Yes. The LCD module uses the same connector and signal protocol as the original Toshiba CRT. No changes to the CNC control, parameters, or wiring are needed. True plug-and-play replacement.

How long does installation take?

10-15 minutes for a technician. Power off, remove old CRT, disconnect cables, mount LCD module, reconnect, power on. No soldering, no special tools, no parameter changes required.

What is the image quality improvement?

Our LCD modules offer 800x600+ resolution (vs 640x400 CRT), 350-450 cd/m2 brightness, 50,000+ hour lifespan. Text is sharper, no flicker, readable in direct sunlight. Significant upgrade over the original CRT.
