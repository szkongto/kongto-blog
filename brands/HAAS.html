About Haas CRT Display Upgrades

Haas Automation is America's largest CNC machine tool builder. CRT displays used in VF Series vertical machining centers with Haas Classic Control are fully discontinued. Common issues include screen dimming, image blurring, color distortion, and flyback transformer failure. OEM LCD upgrade kits are expensive with unpredictable lead times.

Kongto Technology offers Haas CRT-to-LCD upgrades for VF Series 9" mono (amber), 12" color, and 15" LCD displays. Modules use the original 9-pin D-Sub or 15-pin VGA connectors with DC 12V power — plug-and-play with original mounting dimensions. Installation takes 10-15 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions About HAAS CRT to LCD Upgrade

Is HAAS still making CRT displays?

HAAS discontinued CRT display production years ago. OEM replacements are no longer available. Our LCD module is the only practical solution to keep your CNC running with a clear, reliable display.

Will the LCD replacement work with my existing HAAS CNC electronics?

Yes. The LCD module uses the same connector and signal protocol as the original HAAS CRT. No changes to the CNC control, parameters, or wiring are needed. True plug-and-play replacement.

How long does installation take?

10-15 minutes for a technician. Power off, remove old CRT, disconnect cables, mount LCD module, reconnect, power on. No soldering, no special tools, no parameter changes required.

What is the image quality improvement?

Our LCD modules offer 800x600+ resolution (vs 640x400 CRT), 350-450 cd/m2 brightness, 50,000+ hour lifespan. Text is sharper, no flicker, readable in direct sunlight. Significant upgrade over the original CRT.
