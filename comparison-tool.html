Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What if my CRT model is not in the comparison table?▼
Send a photo of your CRT display label to info@cncdisplay.com. Our engineers will confirm compatibility and provide a quote within 24 hours. Custom and OEM solutions (brand logo printing) are also available.
Will the LCD replacement fit the original mounting holes?▼
Yes. All KONGTO LCD replacement modules use the original mounting dimensions and screw holes. They connect via the original signal interface using the existing cable harness — no wiring changes or CNC parameter adjustments needed. Installation takes 10-30 minutes with just a screwdriver.
How much better is LCD vs the original CRT display?▼
LCD replacements deliver 800×600+ resolution (vs 640×400 for CRT), 350-450cd/m² brightness (vs ~200cd/m²), and 50,000+ hour lifespan (vs ~15,000 hours). No flicker, no geometric distortion, 75% lower power consumption, and sharper text for easier machine operation.
What is the warranty policy?▼
All KONGTO LCD modules carry a 2-year free warranty with lifetime technical support. Industrial-grade panels are rated for 50,000 hours (5-7 years continuous operation). Global DHL shipping delivers within 3-7 days.
Why is KONGTO priced lower than Monitech or Industrial Panels?▼
KONGTO ships directly from Shenzhen, eliminating European and North American distributor markups and local warehousing costs. Using the same Sharp/AUO industrial-grade panels, KONGTO delivers equivalent quality at 1/3 to 1/2 the price of Monitech (Germany) or Industrial Panels (USA).
Siemens and Haas have different power supplies — what should I do?▼
Siemens 6FC series older CRTs use AC110V — the KTO-SI6FC module has a built-in AC110V power circuit for direct replacement. Haas VF series uses DC12V — KTO-HAVF9 includes the matching power module. Always confirm your CNC brand and power supply voltage when ordering.
