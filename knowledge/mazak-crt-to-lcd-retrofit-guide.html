Mazak-Specific Considerations

Mazak Mazatrol controls use a 3-row 15-pin connector interface (10 pins used) similar to other Japanese CNC brands. All our Mazak LCD replacements are designed for direct drop-in compatibility — same connector, same mounting dimensions, same video signal timing.

Note: Some Mazak machines have the CRT integrated into the control console rather than mounted separately. Our LCD modules match the original CRT form factor and mounting hole positions.

Installation

  1. Power off the machine and disconnect main power
  2. Remove the CRT by unscrewing the mounting frame (typically 4-6 screws)
  3. Disconnect the 3-row 15-pin video cable and 24V DC power cable
  4. Mount the LCD module using the original screw holes
  5. Connect the cables and power on

Time: 10-15 minutes. Tools: Phillips screwdriver.

Will my Mazak Mazatrol control recognize the LCD?
Yes. The LCD module uses the same video signal format. The control operates without any parameter changes.
