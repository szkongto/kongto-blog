Kongto Technology Engineering Team | Updated July 2026 | 7 min read

Siemens Sinumerik controls power many of the world's machine tools. Their CRT displays, including models from the 810T, 820T, 840C, and 840D series, are now reaching end-of-life. This guide covers LCD replacement for common Siemens Sinumerik CRT displays.

Installation

  1. Power off machine and disconnect main power
  2. Remove CRT (4-6 mounting screws)
  3. Disconnect signal and power cables
  4. Install LCD module and connect cables
  5. Power on — no parameter changes required

Time: 10-15 minutes. Tools: Phillips screwdriver.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the LCD work with Siemens 840D controls?
Yes. Our LCD modules are compatible with Siemens 840D, 810D, 840C, 820T, and 810T controls.
Do I need to update Siemens PLC parameters?
No. The LCD presents the same video signal as the original CRT. No parameter or software changes are needed.
How do I identify my Siemens display model?
The model number is on a label on the CRT housing. Common: 6FC3988-7FA20, 6FC5103, SM0901.
