Common Failure Symptoms

  • MDT962B blank screen — Flyback transformer failure, most common after 5+ years
  • FCUA-CT100 dim display — CRT phosphor wear, brightness cannot be restored
  • BM09DF flickering — Capacitor degradation in power supply circuit
  • MDT925PS intermittent display — Loose internal connectors or cold solder joints

Installation

Installation takes 10-15 minutes and requires only a Phillips screwdriver. Power off the machine, remove the old CRT, connect the LCD module to the existing cables, and power on. No parameter changes needed.
