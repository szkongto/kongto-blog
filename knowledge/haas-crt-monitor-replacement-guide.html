Haas-Specific Notes

Haas CRT monitors use a 9-pin connector (not the 20-pin Honda found on Japanese CNC brands). Our Haas replacement modules include the correct 9-pin cable for plug-and-play installation.

The CRT assembly in Haas machines is typically mounted in a swing-arm enclosure. Remove 4 screws, swap the display module, and reassemble. Total time: 10-15 minutes.

Installation

  1. Power off the Haas machine (main breaker)
  2. Remove the CRT enclosure cover (4 screws)
  3. Disconnect the 9-pin cable and power connector
  4. Mount the LCD module using original bracket
  5. Connect cables and power on
