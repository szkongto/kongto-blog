Mitsubishi MDT962B-1A 9-Inch CRT to LCD Replacement Guide

Industrial CNC Display Solutions | Kongto Technology | Compatibility Reference

Overview

The MDT962B-1A is a 9-inch monochrome CRT used in M3, M310, M64, and E60 Mitsubishi CNC controls. Most common failure: cold-start flickering from capacitor aging and progressive dimming from cathode wear. The industrial LCD replacement uses the original 20-pin connector and DC 24V power. 10-minute plug-and-play installation.

Installation

  1. Power off CNC machine
  2. Remove 4 bezel screws
  3. Disconnect 20-pin connector
  4. Connect new LCD
  5. Mount and power on
