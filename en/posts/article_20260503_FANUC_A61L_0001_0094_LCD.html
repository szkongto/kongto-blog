FANUC A61L-0001-0094 9-inch Color LCD Retrofit Guide

2026-05-03 FANUC CNC Display Solutions

Product Overview

The FANUC A61L-0001-0094 is a 9-inch color CRT display originally used in FANUC CNC systems. As these color CRT displays age, they suffer from color drift, brightness loss, and eventual failure. Our LCD retrofit kit provides a cost-effective, reliable solution with vibrant color reproduction and modern LCD technology while maintaining 100% compatibility.

Why Upgrade to Color LCD?

Color CRT displays have a typical lifespan of 15-20 years. As they age, you may experience:

  • Color accuracy degradation (reds appear orange, blues appear purple)
  • Reduced brightness and contrast (harder to read in bright workshops)
  • Flickering or image distortion
  • Complete failure due to cathode ray tube wear
  • Difficulty obtaining replacement parts (discontinued by manufacturer)

Color LCD technology solves these problems by providing stable, vibrant colors with a lifespan of 50,000+ hours.

Installation Guide

  1. Safety Preparation: Power down the CNC system and disconnect the main power supply. Wait 5 minutes for capacitors to discharge.
  2. Remove Existing CRT: Disconnect the HONDA 20-pin signal cable and power connector. Remove mounting screws and gently slide the CRT unit out.
  3. Install LCD Panel: Position the 9-inch LCD panel using the same mounting points — it's a direct drop-in replacement requiring no modifications.
  4. Connect Cables: Connect the HONDA 20-pin signal cable and DC 24V power. Ensure all connections are secure.
  5. Test and Verify: Restore power and verify display clarity, color accuracy, alarm messages, and menu navigation.
