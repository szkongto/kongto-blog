CRT Repair: The Hidden Costs

A typical CRT repair involves replacing the flyback transformer, high-voltage capacitors, or the video amplifier circuit. The problem: these replacement parts are pulled from 15-20 year old donor units. There are no new CRT components being manufactured anywhere in the world.

Brand-Specific Repair Trends

  • FANUC A61L-0001-0093: High-voltage transformer failure is the most common issue. Repair cost $300-500. Average repair lifespan: 6-10 months.
  • Mitsubishi MDT962B: Capacitor aging in power supply section. Repair cost $200-400. Average repair lifespan: 4-8 months.
  • Mazak CD1472-D1M: Color drift and burn-in. Repair cost $300-600. Burn-in is permanent and cannot be repaired.
  • Siemens 6FC3998-7FA20: Power supply and deflection issues. Repair cost $400-800. Parts increasingly scarce.

Why LCD Lasts Longer

Industrial LCD panels are solid-state devices — no vacuum tube, no high-voltage transformer, no electron gun. The LED backlight is rated for 50,000 hours (5-7 years continuous). There is nothing to burn out the way a CRT's cathode wears down.

The Downtime Factor

Every day a CNC machine is down waiting for a CRT repair costs $500-$2,000 in lost production. The LCD replacement eliminates this recurring downtime entirely.
