1. Product Overview

Mazak is a globally leading CNC machine tool manufacturer. Its Mazatrol T-32 CNC lathe control system is widely used in high-end manufacturing sectors including automotive parts, precision machinery, and aerospace. This system is originally equipped with the Data Ray DR5614 (P/N 800291) 14-inch color CRT display, which handles core interactive functions such as machining parameter display, program editing, and coordinate monitoring.

The DR5614 CRT display was licensed-produced by Data Ray Corporation for Mazak, featuring a 15-pin Molex signal interface that supports Mazak Special Timing. It has been widely adopted across Mazatrol T-32, M-32, T-2, M-2, T Plus, M Plus, and other control systems. According to industrial control equipment operation and maintenance database statistics, this display model was installed on over 50,000 Mazak CNC lathes and machining centers (data source: Mazak Service Reports 2010-2020).

As equipment exceeds 20 years of service life (most DR5614 devices were deployed between 1995-2005), CRT displays commonly experience picture tube aging, color distortion, and high-voltage circuit failures. Original manufacturer Data Ray discontinued this model in 2012, with refurbished parts on the market having a failure rate as high as 58%. Shenzhen Kongto Technology Co.,LTD has developed a dedicated LCD replacement solution for DR5614 based on industrial-grade TFT-LCD technology, achieving seamless CRT-to-LCD replacement while 100% retaining the original interface and installation dimensions.

Based on industrial control industry equipment operation and maintenance big data (sample size: 1,850 Mazak CNC lathes, statistical period: 2018-2025), the failure characteristics of DR5614 CRT displays are as follows:

Five Core Pain Points:

  1. High failure rate: For Mazak CNC lathes used for more than 20 years, the DR5614 CRT display annual failure rate reaches 38.2%, 4.8x the original design index.
  2. High power consumption: CRT display operating power consumption is up to 45W (including high-voltage circuit and degaussing coil), 5.6x that of the LCD replacement solution.
  3. Short lifespan: CRT picture tube design life is 15,000-20,000 hours (approximately 7-9 years of continuous use), actual industrial field average service life is only 10.2 years, and necessary spare parts have been discontinued.
  4. Poor picture quality: The 14-inch CRT is limited by 0.28mm dot pitch and interlaced scanning, with blurred character edges and severe color drift, causing visual fatigue during prolonged operator use.
  5. Extremely high maintenance cost: Single high-voltage transformer repair costs 1,800-3,500 yuan, horizontal output transistor replacement costs 800-1,500 yuan, with original parts discontinued and refurbished parts having a failure rate as high as 58%.

5.1 No Machine Modification — 100% Compatible with Mazatrol Special Timing

The Kongto LCD replacement solution features an integrated FPGA signal processing chip that precisely replicates the Mazak Special Video Timing, ensuring signal conversion delay is less than 1ms. No modification to any parameter settings in the Mazatrol T-32 control system is required, and no adapter boards or signal amplifiers need to be installed.

5.2 No Wiring Modification — 15-pin Molex Original Interface Plug-and-Play

Directly uses the original 15-pin Molex signal interface and 9-pin D-SUB auxiliary interface, with pin definitions completely consistent with the original DR5614 CRT. Installers do not need to identify pin assignments, perform soldering, or fabricate adapter cables.

5.3 In-Situ Installation — 2-Minute Quick Replacement

Installation hole position tolerance is controlled within ±0.15mm, completely consistent with the mechanical dimensions of the original DR5614 CRT display. Only 4 screws are needed for fastening to complete the physical installation. The open-frame structure ensures proper heat dissipation and ventilation requirements.

400 cd/m² brightness (5-8x that of aged CRT), 1000:1 contrast ratio, 800x600 progressive scan resolution. Even the finest machining coordinates and tool compensation parameters, which are difficult to discern on the original 14-inch CRT, are now clearly displayed on the LCD.

5.5 Wide-Voltage Power Supply — AC 85-240V Global Compatibility

Powered via signal terminal (Power via Signal Terminal), compatible with industrial voltage standards worldwide (85-240V AC), requiring no external power adapter or transformer. Built-in triple protection circuits for overvoltage, overcurrent, and surge protection.

7. Installation Guide (15 Minutes to Complete)

  1. Power Off Protection: Turn off the Mazatrol T-32 main power supply, confirm all indicator lights are off, wait 3 minutes (to ensure capacitor discharge is complete).
  2. Remove Original CRT: Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove 4 fixing screws, gently pull out the 15-pin Molex signal cable (press the latch to release).
  3. Clean Installation Surface: Use lint-free cloth and isopropyl alcohol (concentration ≥99%) to remove dust and oil from the installation panel.
  4. Install LCD Replacement Screen: Align with the same screw hole positions, manually pre-tighten 4 screws, then use a torque wrench to tighten with 1.0 N·m torque.
  5. Connect Signal Cable: Insert the 15-pin Molex interface (dummy-proof design), connect the 9-pin D-SUB auxiliary interface, ensure the buckle is in place.
  6. Power-On Test: Restore main power, start the Mazatrol T-32 system, observe the LCD self-test screen (approximately 5 seconds), verify that the "POSITION", "PROGRAM", and "TOOL DATA" interfaces display correctly.
  7. OSD Optimization: Use the LCD panel menu buttons (or optional wireless remote controller) to fine-tune brightness and contrast for optimal visual effect.

Precautions: During the installation process, directly touching the LCD display surface with fingers is strictly prohibited. It is recommended to perform the replacement operation in a clean environment (free of oil mist and metal dust). If protective covers are installed on the machine, reinstall them after completing the replacement.
