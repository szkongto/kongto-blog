FANUC A61L-0001-0093 9-inch CRT to LCD Complete Replacement Guide

Published: 2026-05-26 Kongto Technology Technical Department

FANUC A61L-0001-0093 Original 9-inch Monochrome CRT Display (Typical Aging State After 8-10 Years)

In FANUC CNC systems, the A61L-0001-0093 is one of the most widely deployed 9-inch monochrome CRT display models, extensively paired with FANUC 0 series, 0i series, and OM-D mainstream CNC controllers. This model uses cathode ray tube technology with a designed lifespan of approximately 15,000 hours. Currently, a large number of in-service units have far exceeded their design life, with frequent failures including brightness degradation, screen blurring, and image flickering.

Kongto Technology, leveraging over a decade of industrial display retrofit experience, introduces the A61L-0001-0093 dedicated LCD upgrade module — delivering true plug-and-play replacement: no wiring changes, no parameter adjustments, no structural modifications. This guide covers the complete upgrade workflow across four stages: product identification, signal connection, physical installation, and results demonstration.

Step 1: Product Model Identification and Verification

Before replacing the display, the first task is to confirm that the currently installed CRT model is indeed the A61L-0001-0093. The model label is located on the side or rear nameplate of the display housing.

Verification Checklist

  • Check the label: The nameplate clearly shows "A61L-0001-0093" along with the FANUC original part number KF-M7099H
  • Verify the connectors: Rear panel has a HONDA 20-pin signal connector + DC 24V 2-pin power connector
  • Confirm dimensions: 9-inch monochrome CRT, standard square-frame housing, four-corner screw mounting
  • Exclude similar models: A61L-0001-0090 / 0086 / 0074 appear similar but have different connectors and resolutions — do not confuse

Kongto LCD Upgrade Module Identification

After confirming the old CRT model, take out the Kongto Technology A61L-0001-0093 dedicated LCD upgrade module and verify the following features:

  • Front: Industrial-grade TFT-LCD panel with reinforced glass protective screen, OSD menu control buttons below
  • Rear: HONDA 20-pin compatible signal connector + DC 24V power connector — 100% compatible with original CRT
  • Housing dimensions identical to original CRT — zero mounting hole deviation
  • Module bears Kongto Technology brand label and model identification

Step 2: Signal and Cable Connection Guide

Signal connection is the most critical technical aspect of the entire upgrade. The good news: the Kongto LCD upgrade module uses exactly the same pinout as the original CRT — signal and power cables are one-to-one direct connections. No adapters, no wire cutting, no jumpers required.

2.1 Connector Comparison

Signal ConnectorHONDA 20-pin FemaleHONDA 20-pin Male (Direct Plug)
Power ConnectorDC 24V 2-pin Terminal (from CNC host)DC 12V-24V Wide Input (Auto-adapting)

2.2 Physical Interface Comparison

⚠ Important: The HONDA 20-pin connector features a foolproof latch design — it cannot be inserted in the wrong orientation. A "click" sound indicates the connector is fully locked. Never force the connection.

Step 3: Physical Installation Procedure

The entire physical installation takes approximately 30 minutes and requires only a Phillips screwdriver. Follow these detailed steps:

1 Power Off

  • Turn off the CNC system main power and confirm the electrical cabinet power indicator is off
  • Wait at least 2 minutes to ensure internal capacitors are fully discharged
  • If available, use a multimeter to verify zero voltage at power terminals before proceeding

2 Remove Old CRT

  • Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the 4 corner mounting screws from the display bezel
  • Carefully remove the CRT display from its mounting position — note the CRT is heavy (approx. 2-3 kg), support the bottom with one hand
  • Disconnect the rear HONDA 20-pin signal cable — press the latch to release, then pull straight out
  • Disconnect the DC 24V 2-pin power cable
  • Keep the removed screws safely — they will be reused for the new module

3 Install LCD Upgrade Module

  • Place the Kongto LCD module into the original CRT mounting position, aligning all four mounting holes
  • The LCD module housing has foolproof alignment tabs — it will not fit if oriented incorrectly
  • Tighten the four corner screws with moderate force — do not overtighten

4 Connect Cables

  • Align the HONDA 20-pin signal cable with the LCD module rear signal port — a "click" from the latch confirms proper seating
  • Insert the DC 24V 2-pin power cable into the corresponding power connector
  • Verify both connectors are fully locked in place

5 Power On and Test

  • Restore CNC system main power and power on
  • The LCD module will automatically detect the signal and display — no parameter configuration required
  • Use the OSD menu buttons on the LCD front panel to fine-tune brightness, contrast, and screen position for optimal display
⚠ Note: The entire installation process requires no CNC system parameter modifications, no machine coordinate recalibration, and does not affect machining accuracy. The LCD module is fully transparent and compatible with the CNC system.
