Okuma OSP Series CRT to LCD Replacement Guide

June 2026 | Kongto Technology | Compatibility Matrix

Overview

Okuma OSP (Okuma Sampling Path) CNC controls from the 1990s use proprietary CRT displays in 9-inch, 10.4-inch, and 12.1-inch sizes. The OSP 5000, 5020, and 7000 series are the most common models still in service. Okuma used custom 14-pin and 20-pin connectors that differ from both FANUC and Mitsubishi standards.

Compatible Systems

  • Okuma OSP 5000 (9-inch monochrome CRT)
  • Okuma OSP 5020 (10.4-inch color CRT)
  • Okuma OSP 7000 (12.1-inch color CRT)

Installation

  1. Power off CNC machine
  2. Remove old CRT from housing
  3. Disconnect Okuma-specific connector (14-pin or 20-pin depending on model)
  4. Connect new LCD with matching connector
  5. Mount in original position
  6. Power on and verify

Note on Okuma Connectors

Okuma connectors are proprietary to Okuma and are not interchangeable with FANUC (Honda MR-20M) or Mitsubishi connectors. Always verify your connector type before ordering.
