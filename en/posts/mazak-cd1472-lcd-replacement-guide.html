Mazak CD1472-D1M LCD Replacement Guide

June 2026 | Kongto Technology | Compatibility Matrix

Overview

The Mazak CD1472-D1M is a 10.4-inch color CRT display found in Mazak T-32 and M-32 series CNC controls. This model is particularly prone to color shift and burn-in from displaying the same coordinate information for decades. The LCD replacement provides consistent color accuracy and much higher brightness.

Compatible Systems

  • Mazak T-32 (lathe control)
  • Mazak M-32 (machining center control)
  • Mazak T-PLUS / M-PLUS (with same connector)

Important: Mazak Connector Difference

Mazak CRTs use a 26-pin connector, which is different from FANUC's Honda MR-20M (20-pin). These connectors are physically incompatible — you cannot use a FANUC LCD on a Mazak machine. Always verify the connector type before ordering. The 26-pin connector carries both power (DC 24V) and video signals.

Installation

  1. Power off CNC completely
  2. Remove CRT bezel screws
  3. Disconnect 26-pin connector
  4. Connect 26-pin cable to new LCD
  5. Mount using original screw holes
  6. Power on and verify display

Also Compatible

The same LCD replacement works with: DR5614, C-5470NS, AIQA8DSP40, and MDT-1283B (Mazak T-2/T-3/M-2 controls).
