Relationship Between D9MM-11A and A61L-0001-0093

The D9MM-11A is the internal component designation for the FANUC A61L-0001-0093 9-inch monochrome CRT display. These displays are widely used in FANUC 0i, 16i, and 18i series CNC systems.
