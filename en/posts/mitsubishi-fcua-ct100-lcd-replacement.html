Mitsubishi FCUA-CT100 M500/M520 10.4-Inch LCD Replacement

Industrial CNC Display Solutions | Kongto Technology | Compatibility Reference

Overview

The FCUA-CT100 is a 10.4-inch color CRT for Mitsubishi M500 and M520 controls. Important: uses a 26-pin connector, different from the 20-pin MDT962B/BM09DF. Verify before ordering.

Installation

  1. Power off
  2. Remove old CRT
  3. Disconnect 26-pin
  4. Connect LCD
  5. Power on
