Mitsubishi FCUA-CT100: M500/M520 Series Display

The FCUA-CT100 is a 10.4-inch color CRT display used in Mitsubishi M500 and M520 series CNC controls — Mitsubishi's higher-end 5-axis capable controls.

Connector Note

The FCUA-CT100 uses a 26-pin connector (unlike the 20-pin connector on MDT962B and BM09DF). Verify your connector type before ordering — a 20-pin LCD will not physically fit.
