FANUC A61L-0001-0074: 14-inch Color CRT

The A61L-0001-0074 is a 14-inch color CRT display used in FANUC 0-TC lathe and 0-MC machining center controls. This is one of the larger FANUC CRT models, found primarily on floor-standing CNC machines from the early 1990s.

Key Specifications

Part NumberA61L-0001-0074
Screen14-inch color CRT
ConnectorHonda MR-20M (20-pin)
PowerDC 24V

Installation

The 14-inch CRT weighs approximately 5 kg. Have a second person assist with removal. The replacement LCD is significantly lighter. Installation follows the same plug-and-play process: power off, remove 4 screws, disconnect Honda MR-20M connector, connect new LCD, mount, power on.

Compatible Systems

FANUC 0-TC, 0-MC. Also cross-compatible with A61L-0001-0094 and A61L-0001-0096 (same connector and power).
