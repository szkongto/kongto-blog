FANUC A61L-0001-0074 14-inch Color CRT to LCD Replacement Guide

Industrial CNC Display Solutions | Kongto Technology | Compatibility Reference

Overview

The FANUC A61L-0001-0074 is a 14-inch Color CRT display installed in FANUC 0-TC, 0-MC CNC controls. After 20-30 years of continuous service, these CRTs exhibit the same degradation patterns seen in all aging vacuum tube displays: dimming, flickering, color shift, and focus loss.

The plug-and-play industrial LCD replacement restores full display quality using the original Honda MR-20M (20-pin) connector and DC 24V power supply. Installation takes approximately 10 minutes. No soldering, no wiring modifications, no CNC parameter changes are required.

The 14-inch unit weighs approximately 5 kg. Have a second person assist with removal. The replacement LCD is significantly lighter (~1.5 kg).

Installation Steps

  1. Power off the CNC machine completely and disconnect main power.
  2. Remove old CRT by removing 4 corner screws on the bezel.
  3. Disconnect the Honda MR-20M connector from the back of the CRT unit.
  4. Connect the same cable to the new industrial LCD display.
  5. Mount the LCD using the original screw holes.
  6. Power on and verify display. No parameter changes needed.

Safety Warning

CRT displays contain high-voltage capacitors that can retain a dangerous charge even when the machine is powered off. If you are not experienced with high-voltage electronics, have a qualified technician perform the replacement.
