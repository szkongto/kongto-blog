Can't Decide? Here's Our Honest Recommendation

You Probably Don't Have a Choice

Here's the thing — if your machine came with Siemens SINUMERIK, you're not going to swap it for a FANUC display. The control systems are completely different. The "vs" comparison matters mostly when:

  • You're buying a new machine and choosing between Siemens-controlled vs. FANUC-controlled versions
  • You're retrofitting an old machine and have some flexibility in control choice
  • You're replacing a failed display and wondering if there's a better alternative (short answer: usually not — stick with what your machine was designed for)

If you're just replacing a failed display — call us. We'll tell you exactly which upgrade kit fits your machine, usually in under 2 minutes.
