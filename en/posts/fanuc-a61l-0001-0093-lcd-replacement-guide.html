FANUC A61L-0001-0093 (D9MM-11A) LCD Replacement Guide

Published June 2026 | Kongto Technology

Overview

The FANUC A61L-0001-0093 — also known as D9MM-11A — is a 9-inch amber monochrome CRT display found in FANUC CNC controls manufactured between 1992 and 2005. This is the single most common CRT model in the FANUC ecosystem, used across the 0-TC, 0-MC, 16, 18, and 21 series.

After 20-30 years of service, these CRTs exhibit dim output, flickering, and focus drift that makes reading offsets and program code difficult. The original CRT has been out of production since the early 2000s. The most cost-effective solution is a plug-and-play industrial LCD replacement.

Installation Steps

  1. Power off the CNC machine completely. Lock out power if available.
  2. Remove the four corner screws holding the CRT bezel in place.
  3. Carefully pull the CRT forward. The 9-inch unit weighs approximately 3 kg. Support it with one hand.
  4. Disconnect the Honda MR-20M connector from the back of the CRT. Squeeze the side tabs to release.
  5. Connect the same cable to the new LCD. The connector is identical — no adapter needed.
  6. Mount the LCD using the original screw holes. The replacement is designed for the exact same mounting pattern.
  7. Power on and verify. No parameter changes are needed. The CNC controller cannot tell the difference between the old CRT and the new LCD.
