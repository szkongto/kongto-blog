Mazak CD1472-D1M 10.4-Inch CRT to LCD Replacement

Industrial CNC Display Solutions | Kongto Technology | Compatibility Reference

Overview

The CD1472-D1M is a 10.4-inch color CRT for Mazak T-32 lathe and M-32 machining center controls. Uses a 26-pin connector — physically different from the FANUC Honda MR-20M. Do not cross-connect.

Installation

  1. Power off
  2. Remove CRT
  3. Disconnect 26-pin
  4. Connect LCD
  5. Mount and power on
