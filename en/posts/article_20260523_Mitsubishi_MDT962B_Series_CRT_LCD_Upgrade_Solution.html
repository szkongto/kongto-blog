Self-Diagnosis: Is Your Mitsubishi CRT Failing?

If your Mitsubishi CRT exhibits any of the following symptoms, the CRT has reached end-of-life. Repairing aged CRTs is costly and provides only temporary results — replacement is the only reliable long-term solution.

⏲

Slow Warm-up
Screen takes 5–10 minutes to become visible after power-on; image remains unstable

💡

Very Low Brightness
Overall dim screen; maximum brightness/contrast settings have no effect; screen appears yellowed

➡

Geometric Distortion
Image vertically scrolls or horizontally distorts; image shifts with temperature changes

🔈

Abnormal Noise
High-frequency whine from HV transformer, or burnt smell from aging components

⬤

Partial / Complete Black Screen
Only single horizontal or vertical lines displayed, or no image at all — display completely failed

⚡

Flicker or Jitter
Image unstable with visible flicker; character clarity severely compromised, causing eye strain

The root cause of all these symptoms is CRT electron gun cathode emitter depletion — an irreversible natural aging process. The only lasting solution is LCD replacement. Our TFT LCD solution completely eliminates these problems using modern display technology.
