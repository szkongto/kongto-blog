FANUC A61L-0001-0096: Later-Generation 14-inch Color CRT

The A61L-0001-0096 is a 14-inch color CRT used in FANUC 21i-M and 18i-M controls. These are later-generation FANUC i-series controls from the early 2000s.

Compatible Systems
