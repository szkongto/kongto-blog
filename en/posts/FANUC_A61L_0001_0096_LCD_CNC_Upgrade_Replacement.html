1. Product Overview

The FANUC A61L-0001-0096 is a widely deployed 9-inch monochrome CRT monitor found in FANUC 0i-A, 0i-B, 0i-C, and OM-D series CNC systems. Originally manufactured in the 1990s, these CRT displays have now exceeded 25 years of typical service life. As cathode ray tube technology has been fully phased out and internal components naturally degrade, the failure rate of A61L-0001-0096 monitors has become a critical reliability risk for CNC machine tools worldwide.

Kongto Technology has developed a dedicated LCD replacement module specifically engineered for the A61L-0001-0096, delivering a no-modification, no-rewiring, direct-fit, plug-and-play upgrade solution for FANUC equipment operators around the globe.

6. Installation Guide

The entire upgrade process is straightforward and can be completed by a standard electrician or maintenance technician:

  1. Power off and remove old CRT — Turn off CNC main power, unscrew 4-6 mounting screws, disconnect HONDA 20-pin signal cable and power cable
  2. Clean mounting surface — Wipe the mounting area with alcohol to remove oil and debris
  3. Install LCD module — Place the LCD module in the original CRT cavity, align with mounting holes
  4. Connect cables — Insert HONDA 20-pin connector (align with orientation marker "1"), connect 24V DC power
  5. Secure with screws — Tighten to 4-5 N·m using a torque wrench
  6. Power on and verify — Check display output, use OSD menu to fine-tune brightness, contrast, and position

Important: No CNC system parameters need modification. No machine recalibration is required. Machining accuracy is completely unaffected.
