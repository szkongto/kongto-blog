Industry Pain Points: Aging CRT Monitors in Haas Equipment

Haas Automation CNC machines (VF Series, SL Series, EC Series) are widely used in precision machining workshops worldwide for their reliability and ease of use. However, machines installed 15–20 years ago almost universally use CRT (Cathode Ray Tube) displays, which degrade significantly over time.

Common failure symptoms include:

  • Screen flickering — Unstable image, eye strain for operators
  • Color distortion / monochrome failure — Critical display information becomes unreadable
  • Brightness fade — CRT ages and dims; maximum brightness setting no longer helps
  • High-voltage failure — CRT high-voltage circuits fail, causing intermittent black screen
  • Obsolete spare parts — Original Haas CRT monitors are long out of production; replacements are unavailable or extremely expensive

When a CRT monitor fails, the machine tool stops operating — directly impacting production schedules and delivery commitments.

The LCD Replacement Solution: Non-Destructive Upgrade, Plug-and-Play
