Haas VF Series CRT to LCD Replacement Guide

June 2026 | Kongto Technology | Compatibility Matrix

Overview

Haas VF series CNC machines from the 1990s and early 2000s used 9-inch and 12-inch amber CRTs with standard VGA or 9-pin D-Sub connectors. Unlike Japanese CNCs with proprietary connectors, Haas machines use more standard video interfaces, making LCD replacement relatively straightforward.

Compatible Models

  • Haas VF-0 / VF-1 / VF-2 (9-inch amber CRT, 9-pin D-Sub)
  • Haas VF-3 / VF-4 (12-inch CRT, 15-pin VGA)
  • Haas Classic Control (pre-NGC, all VF series)

Connector Types by Model

Installation

  1. Power off CNC machine
  2. Remove 4 bezel screws
  3. Disconnect video cable and power connector
  4. Connect new LCD (same connectors)
  5. Mount and power on

Note on Haas NGC Machines

Newer Haas NGC (Next Generation Control) machines already use LCD displays. This guide applies to pre-NGC and Classic Control models only.
