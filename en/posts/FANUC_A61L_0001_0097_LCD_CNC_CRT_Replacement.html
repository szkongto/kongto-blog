1. Product Overview

FANUC A61L-0001-0097 is the original part number for FANUC 9-inch monochrome CRT displays, commonly referred to as "FANUC 9-inch green screen" in the industrial automation field. This model has been widely used in FANUC 0i series, 16i series, 18i series and other mainstream CNC systems since the 1990s, with cumulative installed capacity exceeding 150,000 units (data source: FANUC 2015-2020 annual service report).

As service life exceeds the design life (CRT design life 15,000-20,000 hours), original CRT displays commonly experience picture tube aging, severe brightness attenuation, geometric distortion and other failures. According to industrial control equipment operation and maintenance database statistics, the average annual failure rate of A61L-0001-0097 displays used for more than 15 years reaches 41.7%, with a single repair cost of approximately 2,500-4,000 yuan, and original parts were officially discontinued in 2018.

Shenzhen Kongto Technology Co.,LTD has developed a dedicated LCD replacement solution for A61L-0001-0097 based on industrial-grade TFT-LCD technology, achieving lossless upgrade replacement while 100% retaining the original CRT interface and installation dimensions.

7. Installation Guide (30 Minutes to Complete)

  1. Power Off Protection: Turn off the CNC main power supply, cut off 24V DC power, wait 5 minutes (to allow CRT high-voltage capacitor discharge).
  2. Remove Original CRT: Use M4 hex key to remove fixing screws (4-6 pieces), gently pull out the HONDA 20-pin connecting wire (avoid excessive force).
  3. Clean Installation Surface: Use lint-free cloth and isopropyl alcohol (concentration ≥99%) to remove dust, oil, and old silicone from the installation surface.
  4. Install LCD Replacement Screen: Align with the same screw hole positions, manually pre-tighten 4 screws, confirm even gaps, then use a torque wrench to tighten with 0.8 N·m torque.
  5. Connect Signal Wire: Insert the HONDA 20-pin interface (dummy-proof design, cannot be inserted in wrong direction), ensure the buckle is in place.
  6. Power-on Test: Restore 24V power, observe the LCD self-test screen (approximately 3-5 seconds), after confirming normal display, execute "SYSTEM" → "DISPLAY TEST" self-test program in the CNC control panel.

Precautions: During the installation process, directly touching the LCD display surface with fingers is strictly prohibited (body oils will cause permanent spots). If accidental contact occurs, use dedicated LCD cleaner and microfiber cloth to gently wipe.
