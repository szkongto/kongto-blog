Siemens 6FC3998-7FA20 SINUMERIK 810/820 LCD Replacement [AC110V]

Industrial CNC Display Solutions | Kongto Technology | Compatibility Reference

Overview

The 6FC3998-7FA20 is a 12.1-inch monochrome CRT for SINUMERIK 810/820 controls. Widely used in European factories since the late 1980s.

CRITICAL: AC 110V Power

Siemens SINUMERIK CRTs use AC 110V — NOT DC 24V like Japanese CNCs. Connecting a DC-powered LCD will cause permanent damage. Always measure your supply voltage before connecting any replacement.

Connector: DB-25

Uses a 25-pin D-Sub connector — physically different from all Japanese CNC connectors (Honda MR-20M, 20/26-pin).

Installation

  1. Verify AC 110V with multimeter
  2. Power off CNC
  3. Remove old CRT
  4. Disconnect DB-25
  5. Connect LCD
  6. Power on
