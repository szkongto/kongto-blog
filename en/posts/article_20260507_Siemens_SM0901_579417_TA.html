Siemens SM0901-579417-TA: 9-Inch Industrial Monochrome CRT Monitor

The Siemens SM0901-579417-TA (Siemens part number 6FC3998-7FA20) is a 9-inch monochrome CRT monitor widely used in Siemens SINUMERIK CNC systems. For over two decades, this monitor has served as the primary display interface for countless Siemens-equipped machining centers and CNC lathes worldwide.

As these monitors age (many installed in the late 1990s and early 2000s), CRT technology limitations become increasingly apparent: electron gun aging, phosphor degradation, high-voltage transformer fatigue, and mechanical wear all contribute to declining display quality and reliability.

Self-Diagnosis: Is Your SM0901-579417-TA Failing?

If your Siemens monitor shows any of the symptoms below, the CRT has reached end-of-life. Repairing aging CRTs is costly and yields only short-term results — replacement is the only reliable long-term solution.

⏰

Slow warm-up
Takes 5–10 minutes after power-on before the screen becomes readable; image remains unstable

🌕

Excessive dimming
Overall image is dim; maxing out brightness and contrast does not help; screen appears yellowish

〰

Image distortion
Wavy lines, flickering, or partial display failure; text becomes difficult to read

⚡

Intermittent power loss
Monitor randomly turns off and on; requires multiple power cycles to start
