Siemens SM0901: NFP 579417 TA

The SM0901 is a 12.1-inch monochrome CRT manufactured by NFP (part number 579417 TA) for Siemens SINUMERIK 810M controls.

Critical Warning: AC110V Power

Like all Siemens SINUMERIK 810/820 series CRTs, the SM0901 uses AC 110V power — NOT DC24V. Connecting a DC-powered LCD to this supply will permanently damage the display. Always measure your supply voltage before connecting.
