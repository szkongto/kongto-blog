Mitsubishi BM09DF E60 Color TFT LCD Replacement

Industrial CNC Display Solutions | Kongto Technology | Compatibility Reference

Overview

The BM09DF is a 9-inch color TFT display for Mitsubishi E60 CNC controls. Critical difference from MDT962B: requires a color LCD replacement, not monochrome.

Installation

  1. Power off
  2. Remove old display
  3. Disconnect 20-pin
  4. Connect color LCD
  5. Mount and power on
