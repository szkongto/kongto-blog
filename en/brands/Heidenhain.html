FAQ

Q: What's the difference between BE211 and BE411?

A: Both are 9-inch mono CRT displays. BE211 is for TNC 310/320 series, BE411 for TNC 410/415 series. Connector pinout differs slightly — select based on your control model.

Q: Will LCD upgrade affect machining accuracy?

A: No. The LCD module only replaces the display function — it does not interact with CNC control loops. No parameters or accuracy are affected.
