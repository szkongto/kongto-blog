About FANUC CRT Display Upgrades

FANUC is the world's largest CNC system manufacturer. Its CRT displays are widely used across the 0 Series, 0i Series, 16i/18i/21i Series, and Power Mate controls. Since CRT production has been fully discontinued, in-service displays increasingly suffer from dimming, blurring, color distortion, and startup failures.

Kongto Technology offers complete FANUC CRT-to-LCD upgrade solutions covering A61L-0001-0074 through 0097 and D9MM-11A variants. All modules use industrial-grade TFT-LCD panels with 350-450cd/m² brightness, 50,000+ hour lifespan, and the original Honda MR-20M connector for plug-and-play installation — no CNC parameter changes needed.
