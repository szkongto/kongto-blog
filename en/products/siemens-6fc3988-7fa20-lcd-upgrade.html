Common SIEMENS 6FC3988 7FA20 CRT Display Failures

The SIEMENS 6FC3988 7FA20 is a CRT display found on Siemens Sinumerik 810/820/840/880 controls from the 1980s–1990s.

  • Blank screen / no display — Siemens Sinumerik CRT flyback failure. The control runs but the screen is dark.
  • Flickering or unstable image — Capacitor aging in the horizontal deflection circuit.
  • Dim display — CRT phosphor wear after 20+ years of continuous operation.
  • Intermittent display / warm-up required — Cold solder joints or failing electrolytics. The display may take 5–15 minutes to stabilize.

All CRT hardware failures. The Siemens control logic is typically unaffected — our LCD module restores display without parameter changes.
