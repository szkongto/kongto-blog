Common FANUC A61L-0001-0094 CRT Display Failures

The A61L-0001-0094 is a 14-inch color CRT used on FANUC Series 6, 10, 11, and 12 CNC systems from the 1980s–1990s. As a color CRT, it is more complex than monochrome units and develops distinct failure patterns:

  • Color shift or missing one color — The red, green, or blue drive transistor on the CRT neck board fails. One channel drops out, leaving a pink, cyan, or yellow-tinted image.
  • Dim or blurry image — The CRT gun emission degrades with age. Unlike monochrome CRTs, color CRTs have three guns — if one weakens faster, the color balance shifts along with overall dimness.
  • No display / dead CRT — The flyback transformer or horizontal deflection circuit fails. The CNC powers up and runs, but the screen stays dark.
  • Geometry distortion — The image is stretched, pinched, or rotated. Aging capacitors in the deflection yoke circuit cause the raster to distort as the machine warms up.
  • Intermittent display — Cold solder joints on the CRT neck board or power supply dry out over 20+ years. The display flickers or only appears after 5–15 minutes of warm-up.

These are all CRT hardware failures. The FANUC CNC control itself typically functions correctly — swapping the CRT for our LCD module restores a clear, stable display without modifying the control.

Color CRT Signal Wiring (HONDA 20-Pin)

Unlike the monochrome A61L variants that use a single video channel, the A61L-0001-0094 color CRT carries separate R/G/B signals plus composite sync. Our LCD module handles both color and monochrome inputs on the same connector — plug and play, no jumper changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

The LCD is 12.1-inch but my original CRT is 14-inch. Will it fit the bezel opening?

Yes. CRT sizes measure the entire glass face including the masked bezel area, whereas LCD sizes measure the visible panel. The viewing area is comparable. The LCD module mounts using the original CRT screw holes and fits within the same panel cutout. An adapter bracket is included for certain cabinet variants.

Does this work with FANUC Series 6 controls from the 1980s?

Yes. The A61L-0001-0094 was originally paired with Series 6, 10, 11, and 12 controls. The LCD module reproduces the same video timing as the original CRT. No parameter changes or software updates are needed regardless of the control generation.

The CRT shows one missing color (e.g. no red). Will the LCD have the same problem?

No. A missing color on the CRT is caused by a failed drive transistor on the CRT neck board, not by the CNC control losing that signal. The LCD reads R/G/B directly from the control's video output. If the control outputs all three colors correctly, the LCD displays them correctly.

Can a maintenance technician install this, or do we need a FANUC specialist?

Any technician familiar with opening a CNC cabinet can complete the installation in 10–15 minutes. Connectors are keyed (cannot be inserted incorrectly), mounting uses original screw locations, and there are no jumpers or adjustments needed. No FANUC-specific training or special tools required.
