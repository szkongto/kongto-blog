Common Toshiba D9MM-11A CRT Display Failures

The D9MM-11A is a 9-inch monochrome CRT (also known as FANUC A61L-0001-0093) used on FANUC 0-Series and 0i-Series controls. Same failure patterns as its FANUC-branded counterpart:

  • Blank screen / no display — CRT heater or flyback failure. The CNC runs but the screen stays dark.
  • Dim display — CRT phosphor degradation after 15–30 years of use.
  • Flickering image — Capacitor aging in the deflection circuit.
  • Burn-in / ghosting — Permanent phosphor wear from static screen elements.

All CRT hardware failures. The CNC control is typically functional — our LCD module restores the display without control modifications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is Toshiba D9MM-11A the same as FANUC A61L-0001-0093?
A: Yes. They are the same 9-inch monochrome CRT. Toshiba/Totoku manufactured it under part number D9MM-11A, and FANUC sourced it as A61L-0001-0093. The LCD replacement module is identical for both.
Q: Which FANUC controls use the D9MM-11A display?
A: FANUC 0-Series (0-MA, 0-TC), 0i-A/B/C/D Series, and OM-D controls. It is the standard 9-inch monochrome display for these systems.
