Common FANUC A61L-0001-0072 CRT Display Failures

The A61L-0001-0072 is a 9-inch monochrome CRT found on FANUC Series 6M/6T controls from the 1980s. After 30+ years of operation, these units experience well-known failure modes:

  • Blank screen / no display — CRT heater burnout or flyback transformer failure. The most common failure. The CNC control still operates (spindle runs, keys respond) but the screen is dark.
  • Dim display — cannot read in daylight — The P31 phosphor has degraded from prolonged use. Even at maximum brightness, the display is unreadable under normal shop lighting.
  • Flickering / unstable image — Electrolytic capacitors in the horizontal deflection circuit have dried out. The image shakes, rolls, or collapses intermittently.
  • Vertical or horizontal collapse — One of the deflection yoke drivers fails, compressing the display to a bright line.
  • Burn-in / ghost image — Static screen elements (position readout, menu bars) are permanently etched into the phosphor after decades of use.

All of these are CRT hardware failures. The FANUC control electronics are typically still functional. Replacing just the display with our LCD module restores full readability without replacing the entire control.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will this LCD work on both Series 6M (milling) and 6T (turning) controls?

Yes. Both 6M and 6T controls output the same video format through the A61L-0001-0072 display interface. The LCD module reads the video signal and sync timings directly from the control — it is compatible with both machine types.

The Series 6 control is very old. Will the LCD work with its low-resolution video output?

Yes. The LCD module is designed to lock onto the 640×400 resolution and 15–18 kHz horizontal scan rate used by FANUC Series 6 controls. It will display the image correctly at the native resolution without any scaling artifacts.

The CRT is completely dead. Is the CNC control still functional?

In most cases, yes. The CRT is a separate display component powered by its own high-voltage supply. When the CRT fails, the CNC control continues to generate video output — you just cannot see it. Our LCD module connects directly to the control's video driver board and will display the signal immediately.

Installation difficulty for a shop-floor maintenance technician?

15 minutes. Remove the CRT housing (4 screws), unplug the two connectors (HONDA 20-pin + DC power), mount the LCD, reconnect the same connectors, and power on. No soldering, no parameter changes, no special tools.
