Mazak 14"CRTCommon Failures

Mazak 14"CRTis a Color CRT display for Mazak Mazatrol control systems，Common failures after 20+ years of service:：

  • Black screen or dim display — CRT flyback transformer or phosphor failure。
  • Color shift — CRT socket driver board failure。
  • Flickering/Geometric distortion — Deflection circuit capacitor aging。

All of The above are CRT hardware failures..Mazak control systems (Mazatrol) typically function normally — Our LCD module directly restores the display。
