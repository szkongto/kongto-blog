Common Siemens 6FC5103 CRT Display Failures

The 6FC5103-0AB01 is a CRT display unit used on Siemens Sinumerik controls. Like all industrial CRTs, it develops characteristic failure modes after 20+ years:

  • Blank screen / no display — Flyback transformer failure. The control powers up but the display remains dark.
  • Dim or blurry image — CRT phosphor degradation or gun emission loss.
  • Flickering / unstable raster — Capacitor aging in the deflection circuit.
  • Distorted geometry — The image stretches, compresses, or rotates as the yoke driver components drift out of spec.

These are CRT hardware failures. The Siemens control is typically functional — our LCD module restores display without control modifications.

Signal Wiring (DB-25 Connector)

The Siemens 6FC5103 uses a DB-25 (25-pin D-sub) connector for video and sync — a different interface from the HONDA 20-pin used on most FANUC and Mitsubishi displays. Our LCD module includes the matching DB-25 connector.
