Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does the 6FC5103-0AB01 LCD upgrade require Siemens software changes?
A: No. This is a hardware-only drop-in replacement. The LCD module uses the same DB-25 signal pinout and DC 24V power as the original 6FC5103 CRT. No PLC, NC, or HMI parameter changes are required.
Q: My 840D display is very dim — should I repair the CRT or replace?
A: CRT dimming is typically caused by tube aging (phosphor degradation) or flyback transformer failure. Repairing a 10-20 year old Siemens CRT is often more expensive than replacing with our LCD module, which provides 2x+ brightness and eliminates future CRT failures.
Q: What's the difference between 6FC5103-0AB01 and 6FC3998-7FA20?
A: 6FC5103 is for newer SINUMERIK 840D systems with DB-25 video interface. 6FC3998 is for older SINUMERIK 850/880 systems with a different connector. Contact us to confirm correct matching for your specific machine.
