Common OKUMA OSP CRT CRT Display Failures

The OKUMA OSP CRT is a CRT display found on Okuma OSP-controlled CNC machines.

  • Blank screen — Okuma OSP CRT flyback or heater failure.
  • Dim / unreadable display — CRT phosphor wear after extended use.
  • Flickering or rolled image — Capacitor failure in the sync circuit.

CRT hardware failure. The Okuma OSP control is typically functional — our LCD restores display.
