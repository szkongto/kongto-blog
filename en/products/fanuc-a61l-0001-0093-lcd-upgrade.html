Common FANUC A61L-0001-0093 Display Problems

The FANUC A61L-0001-0093 is a 9-inch monochrome CRT display used extensively on FANUC 0-series and 0i-series CNC systems from the 1980s through early 2000s. After 15–30 years of operation, these CRTs develop characteristic failure modes:

  • Blank screen / no display — The CRT heater fails or the flyback transformer breaks down. Most common failure. The CNC still operates (keys beep, spindle moves), but the screen stays dark.
  • Dim display / low brightness — The CRT phosphor has degraded from extended use. Even with max brightness adjustment, the display is too dim to read under shop floor lighting.
  • Flickering or shaky image — Sync circuit components (capacitors, ICs) drift out of spec. The image jitters or scrolls vertically.
  • Horizontal or vertical line collapse — One of the deflection circuits fails. The image compresses to a bright horizontal or vertical line.
  • Burned-in image / ghosting — Static elements (position readout, mode display) become permanently visible as a ghost image on the phosphor.

All of these are CRT hardware failures. The CNC control itself is typically still functional — replacing just the display with our LCD module restores full visibility without replacing the entire control system.

Does the LCD replacement work with all FANUC CNC models that use the A61L-0001-0093 CRT?

Yes. The LCD module uses the same HONDA 20-pin connector and TTL-level signal protocol as the original CRT. It is a drop-in replacement for any machine originally equipped with the A61L-0001-0093, including FANUC 0, 0-Mate, 0i-A/B/C/D, 16i, 18i, and 21i series controls. Will I lose any CNC functionality after replacing the CRT with LCD?

No. The LCD module presents exactly the same video signal interface to the CNC control as the original CRT. All on-screen displays (position, program, offset, parameter, alarm, diagnostic screens) function identically. The LCD uses the same resolution timing and sync rates. The only difference is a clearer, brighter, and flicker-free image.

What is the installation time and difficulty level?

Installation takes 10–15 minutes for a technician familiar with CNC cabinets. Steps: (1) power off the machine, (2) remove the four screws securing the CRT housing, (3) disconnect the HONDA 20-pin connector and 24V power wires, (4) mount the LCD module in the same opening, (5) reconnect the same connectors, (6) power on and verify. No soldering, no parameter changes, no special tools.

The LCD provides 800×600 resolution (vs 640×400 on the original CRT), 350–450 cd/m² brightness (vs 200 cd/m²), and a 50,000+ hour lifespan (vs ~15,000 hours for the CRT). Text and characters are sharper with no geometric distortion. There is no flicker at any refresh rate. The display is readable even under direct sunlight on the shop floor.
