Common Toshiba D14CM-01A CRT Failures

The D14CM-01A is a 14-inch color CRT (also FANUC A61L-0001-0096) used on FANUC 16/18/20/21 series controls:

  • Color shift or missing one color — CRT neck board driver transistor failure.
  • Dim / blurry — CRT gun emission degradation. Three guns age unevenly.
  • No display — Flyback transformer failure. CNC runs but screen is dark.
  • Geometry distortion — Capacitor aging in the deflection circuit.

CRT hardware failures. The CNC control is typically functional — our LCD restores display.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is D14CM-01A the same as A61L-0001-0096?
