Price & Ordering FAQ

Q: What is the price range for CRT to LCD upgrade kits?

Our LCD upgrade kits typically cost 30%-50% of the original CRT monitor price. For example, the FANUC A61L-0001-0093 original CRT is discontinued with highly unstable market prices, while our LCD upgrade kit is about 40% of the original price, with stable supply and 2-year warranty.

Q: What is the minimum order quantity?

We accept orders from 1 unit. We understand many customers want to test 1 unit for compatibility verification before bulk purchasing. Single unit and volume pricing differ — please inquire for details.

Q: Does the price include installation service?

Standard kit price does not include on-site installation. However, we provide: ① Detailed illustrated installation guide; ② Remote video installation guidance; ③ On-site installation available upon request (additional cost). Most customers complete installation themselves without extra service.

Q: How do I confirm which kit fits my machine?

Three ways: ① Use our Compatibility Matrix tool to self-check; ② Send your machine model and display photo to us, our engineer will confirm within 2 hours; ③ If your model is not in the compatibility list, we can provide custom development service.

Q: What are the payment and delivery terms?

We support multiple payment methods: Alipay, WeChat Pay, bank transfer, PayPal (international customers). Domestic customers: 3-5 business days delivery. International customers: 7-10 business days (including shipping). Formal invoice available.

Q: Can I return the kit if it's not compatible?

We provide a 30-day no-reason return & exchange guarantee. If the product has quality issues or is incompatible, you can apply for a full refund or free replacement with the correct model. Buyer bears return shipping (except for quality issues).
