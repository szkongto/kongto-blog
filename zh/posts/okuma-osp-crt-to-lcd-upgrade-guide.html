This guide covers monochrome and color CRT replacements for Okuma machines, with specifications for MDT-1005 and related models.

Installation

1. Power Down

Turn off OSP system. Disconnect from mains. Wait 2 minutes.

2. Remove CRT

Unscrew housing. Disconnect signal cable and DC 24V power.

3. Install LCD

Mount into original position. Holes align. Use original screws.

4. Test

Power on. LCD displays automatically. Adjust with OSD.
