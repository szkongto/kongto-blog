Siemens Sinumerik 810T/820T CRT to LCD Upgrade — Complete Guide

Published: July 12, 2026 | Updated: July 12, 2026 | 作者: szkongto01

Overview

The Siemens Sinumerik 810T and 820T are among the most widely used CNC controls in European machine tools — found on Deckel Maho, Hermle, DMG, and many other German-made machines. These controls use 9-inch monochrome or color CRT displays that are now well past their design life. If your 810T or 820T display is fading, flickering, or dead, an LCD upgrade is the most cost-effective solution.

⚠ Important: Unlike FANUC and Mitsubishi which use DC 24V, Siemens Sinumerik CRTs operate on AC 110V or AC 220V. Our LCD modules include a built-in wide-range AC power supply compatible with both voltages. Confirm your machine voltage before ordering.

Identifying Your Siemens CRT Model

To find the correct LCD replacement for your machine:

  1. Check the CRT label. Look for a sticker on the CRT bezel or rear housing. The OEM part number is usually printed as 6FC3988-7FA20, 6FC3988-7FA21, SM0901-579417TA, or 6FC4600-0AR04.
  2. Check your control model. The Sinumerik control type is displayed on the machine nameplate or the control panel: 810T (GA2 or GA3), 820T, 810D, 840D.
  3. Take a photo of the CRT label and send it to info@cncdisplay.com — we will confirm compatibility within 24 hours.

Installation Guide

Installing the LCD upgrade on a Siemens 810T or 820T takes approximately 30 minutes. No special tools, no CNC parameter changes, no wiring modifications.

Tools Required

  • Phillips screwdriver (#2)
  • Flat-head screwdriver (for wire terminals)
  • Voltage tester (safety check)

Step-by-Step

  1. Power off completely. Turn off the Sinumerik control using the main power switch. Unplug the AC power cord. Wait 3 minutes for internal capacitors to discharge. Use a voltage tester to confirm zero voltage at the CRT power input.
  2. Remove the CRT bezel. The CRT is mounted behind a front bezel secured by 4 screws. Remove the bezel carefully.
  3. Disconnect cables. Unplug the DB-25 signal cable from the CRT rear. Disconnect the AC power wires (note or photograph the wire positions — neutral, live, ground).
  4. Remove CRT. The CRT unit is typically held by 4 mounting screws in a metal bracket. Support the CRT weight (~6 kg) as you remove the last screw, then lift it out.
  5. Mount LCD module. Position the LCD module in the same bracket. The mounting holes align with the standard Siemens CRT mounting pattern — no drilling required. Secure with the original screws.
  6. Connect cables. Connect the DB-25 signal cable (keyed, fits one way only). Connect AC power wires (line, neutral, ground). The LCD module has a standard 3-pin terminal block — match wires to markings.
  7. Test. Reconnect AC power and turn on the machine. The LCD should display the Sinumerik boot screen immediately. Use the OSD menu (buttons on the LCD) to adjust brightness and contrast if needed.

Common Issues and Troubleshooting

No display after installation

  • Verify AC power is present at the LCD module input (use voltage tester)
  • Check DB-25 connection — ensure it is fully seated and screws are tightened
  • Confirm the CNC control is booting (listen for relay clicks, check control panel LEDs)

Display flickers or scrolls

  • The LCD auto-detects the video signal format. If scrolling occurs, the control may be outputting a non-standard refresh rate. Contact us for configuration assistance.

Display too dim or too bright

  • Use the LCD OSD (on-screen display) buttons to adjust brightness and contrast. The OSD is accessed via buttons on the LCD bezel.

Green rectangle on screen

  • This is a known Siemens 810T/820T boot behavior where the control outputs a green background before loading the operating system. It is normal and disappears once the control software loads.
⚠ Safety note: Siemens Sinumerik CRTs contain high-voltage components (anode at 10-15 kV). Do not attempt to open or repair the CRT yourself. Discharge time: at least 3 minutes after power-off.
