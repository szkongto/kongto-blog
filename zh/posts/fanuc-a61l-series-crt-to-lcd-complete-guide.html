FANUC A61L-0001 Series CRT to LCD Upgrade Guide

Published: June 27, 2026 | Category: FANUC LCD Retrofit | 作者: szkongto01

FANUC is the world's largest CNC system manufacturer, and its A61L-0001 series CRT displays were used across millions of machines worldwide — from the Series 0 and 0i to the 16i/18i/21i controls. These CRTs are now fully discontinued, and in-service units are failing at an accelerating rate.

This guide covers every A61L-0001 model from 0072 through 0097, including the D9MM-11A variant. You'll find model-specific specifications, compatible CNC system lists, a complete cross-reference table, pricing, installation instructions, and links to dedicated product pages for each model.

⚠️ Quick Reference: All FANUC A61L-0001 CRT displays use the Honda MR-20M 20-pin connector and DC 24V power. Our LCD modules replace them directly — plug-and-play, no wiring modifications, no CNC parameter changes. Prices range from $155 to $350. All include 18-month warranty.

Installation Guide

1. Power Down

Turn off CNC main power. Remove CRT housing (4 screws). Disconnect Honda 20-pin signal cable and DC 24V power.

2. Install LCD

Place LCD module into original CRT position. Holes align 100%. Secure with original screws. No drilling needed.

3. Connect

Plug Honda 20-pin cable into LCD. Connect DC 24V. Keyed connectors prevent incorrect insertion.

4. Power On

Restore power. LCD displays automatically. Adjust via OSD buttons. Total time: ~30 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I identify which A61L model I have?
A: Check the label on the back or side of your CRT monitor. The model number is printed as "A61L-0001-XXXX". You can also cross-reference your FANUC CNC system model against our compatibility matrix.
Q: Can I upgrade a color CRT to LCD?
A: Yes. Models 0074, 0094, 0096, and 0097 are color CRTs (14-inch). They are replaced with 12.1-inch color TFT-LCD modules. Same Honda 20-pin interface, same DC 24V power.
Q: What if my model isn't listed?
