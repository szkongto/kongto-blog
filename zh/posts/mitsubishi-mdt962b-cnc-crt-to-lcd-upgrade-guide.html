Mitsubishi MDT962B Series CRT to LCD Upgrade Guide

Published: June 27, 2026 | Category: Mitsubishi LCD Retrofit | 作者: szkongto01

Mitsubishi MDT962B series CRT monitors are among the most widely used displays on Mitsubishi MELDAS and M-series CNC controls. Found on M64, E60, M50, M500, M520, and M3/M310 machining centers, these 9-inch monochrome CRTs are now failing at increasing rates as their components age beyond their design life.

This guide covers the complete MDT962B family (including MDT962B-1A and MDT962B-4A), as well as compatible models BM09DF, FCUA-CT100, MDT947B, and MDT1283B. You'll find model cross-references, interface specifications, installation steps, and pricing for each option.

⚠️ Quick Answer: Mitsubishi MDT962B, BM09DF, and FCUA-CT100 CRTs are replaced with industrial TFT-LCD modules using the original 10-pin flat cable connector. Plug-and-play installation, 15-30 minutes. each — outright sale, no core exchange. 2-year warranty.

Installation Steps

1. Power Down

Turn off CNC main power. Disconnect the machine from mains. Wait 1 minute for capacitor discharge.

2. Remove CRT

Unscrew the 4 mounting screws. Slide the CRT housing forward. Disconnect the 10-pin flat cable and DC power wires.

3. Install LCD

Place the LCD module into the original mounting frame. Holes align 100%. Secure with original screws.

4. Connect Cables

Plug the 10-pin flat cable into the LCD module connector. Connect DC power. Keyed connectors prevent errors.

5. Power On

Restore power and start the CNC. The LCD displays automatically. Use OSD buttons to adjust brightness.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is the 10-pin connector the same across all Mitsubishi models?
A: Yes. MDT962B, MDT962B-1A, MDT962B-4A, BM09DF, and FCUA-CT100 all use the same 10-pin flat ribbon cable interface. Our LCD modules are designed to match this connector directly.
Q: What voltage does the Mitsubishi MDT962B use?
A: The MDT962B series runs on DC 12V or DC 24V depending on the specific machine configuration. Our LCD modules auto-detect and accept both voltages.
Q: Do I need to modify the CNC parameters after installation?
A: No. The LCD module uses the same video signal format as the original CRT. No parameter changes are needed on the Mitsubishi CNC control.
Q: How do I identify which MDT962B variant I have?
A: Check the label on the back of the CRT. MDT962B-1A and MDT962B-4A are sub-variants with different mounting bracket designs. All three use the same LCD module.
