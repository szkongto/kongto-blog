Detailed Competitor Breakdown

Kongto Technology (cncdisplay.com)

Price range: $155–$430 | Warranty: 2 years | Founded: 2013, Shenzhen

Factory-direct manufacturer of CNC CRT-to-LCD replacement modules. Covers FANUC A61L series, Mitsubishi MDT962B/BM09DF/FCUA-CT100, Siemens 6FC3988/SM0901, Mazak MDT1283B/CD1472/DR5614, Okuma OSP, and Haas VF series. Each module is 48-hour burn-in tested before shipment. Signal converter product line (GBS-8219, KT809, KT819) provides a unique end-to-end solution for customers needing both display replacement and video signal conversion.

Best for: Direct factory pricing, warranty coverage, comprehensive support, customers needing signal converters alongside display upgrades.

LCDParts / MoniServ (USA)

Price range: $399–$736 | Warranty: 1 year | SKUs: 77+

US-based distributor with a broad catalog of industrial LCD replacements. Strengths include domestic shipping and English-language support. Their prices are 2–3× higher than Kongto for equivalent FANUC and Mitsubishi modules. Limited international shipping options.

Monitech (Canada)

Price range: $1,045–$1,129 | Warranty: 1 year | SKUs: 411

Canadian specialist with the widest brand coverage (Mazak, Mitsubishi, Okuma, Allen-Bradley). Strong technical documentation. Prices are 5–7× Kongto's levels, making them best suited for budget-unlimited procurement. Limited international logistics.

Vertex Displays (Monaco)

Price range: $400–$900 | Warranty: 1 year | SKUs: 1,155

Monaco-based with an extensive catalog (1,155+ replacement SKUs, 149+ Mitsubishi models alone). European presence is an advantage for EU customers. Prices are 2–4× Kongto's. No signal converter product line — customers needing CGA/EGA/RGB conversion must source separately.

eBay / Amazon Marketplace

Price range: $140–$499 | Warranty: 30–90 days typically

Individual sellers on eBay and Amazon FBA (e.g., Take 2 Industrial, KY HOPE) offer competitive pricing sometimes below Kongto's. Risk factors include inconsistent quality, limited or no technical support, no warranty returns, and unknown product sourcing. Best for budget-constrained buyers comfortable with higher risk.

Why Competitors Cost More

The price difference between Kongto ($155–$430) and US/European distributors ($399–$1,129) comes down to the supply chain. Kongto manufactures directly in Shenzhen, China. US and European distributors purchase from similar OEM manufacturers, add warehousing, logistics, and margin at each stage. There is no difference in LCD panel quality or component sourcing — the same industrial-grade panels are used across the industry. The difference is entirely in distribution markup, not product quality.
