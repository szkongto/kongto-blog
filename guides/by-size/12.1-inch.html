Why Upgrade from CRT to LCD?

  • Brighter display — 350-450 cd/m² vs 100-150 cd/m² for aged CRTs
  • Higher resolution — 800×600 vs 640×400 for most CRTs
  • Longer lifespan — 50,000+ hours vs 10,000-15,000 hours for CRTs
  • Lower power consumption — LCD uses 70% less power than CRT
  • Plug-and-play — No CNC parameter changes, no rewiring needed

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What CRT size does a 12.1-inch LCD replace?
12.1-inch LCD modules replace 14-inch CRT displays (both monochrome and color). This is our largest standard replacement size.

Q: Are the 12.1-inch LCDs color?
Some models are available in color (FANUC 0096/0097), while others are high-resolution monochrome. All provide 1024×768 resolution with 450 cd/m² brightness.
