Why Upgrade from CRT to LCD?

  • Brighter display — 350-450 cd/m² vs 100-150 cd/m² for aged CRTs
  • Higher resolution — 800×600 or 1024×768 vs 640×400 for most CRTs
  • Longer lifespan — 50,000+ hours vs 10,000-15,000 hours for CRTs
  • Lower power consumption — LCD uses 70% less power than CRT
  • No maintenance — No flyback transformer, no high voltage, no convergence adjustments
  • Plug-and-play — No CNC parameter changes, no rewiring needed

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I know which size LCD I need?
A: Check the CRT size marking on your original display or measure the screen diagonally. Our compatibility matrix can also help identify the correct replacement by your CNC model number.

Q: Will an LCD work with my old CNC controller?
A: Yes. Our LCD modules are designed to match the original CRT's video timing and connector pinout. They work with all standard CNC controllers without modification.

Q: Is the image quality better than the original CRT?
A: Significantly. LCDs provide sharper text, higher brightness, better contrast, and no geometric distortion. Color LCDs also support higher resolution than color CRTs.
