Why Upgrade from CRT to LCD?

  • Brighter display — 350-450 cd/m² vs 100-150 cd/m² for aged CRTs
  • Higher resolution — 800×600 vs 640×400 for most CRTs
  • Longer lifespan — 50,000+ hours vs 10,000-15,000 hours for CRTs
  • Lower power consumption — LCD uses 70% less power than CRT
  • Plug-and-play — No CNC parameter changes, no rewiring needed

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What CRT size does a 10.4-inch LCD replace?
10.4-inch LCD modules replace 12-inch CRT displays. The viewable image area is comparable because CRT measurements include the tube bezel.

Q: Is the 10.4-inch LCD size common?
Yes. It is the second most common size after 8-inch. Used on larger FANUC systems (0074/0094), Matsushita displays, and some Siemens SINUMERIK models.
