CNC TTL RGB Signal Pinout Guide
FANUC, Mitsubishi, Siemens CRT to LCD Wiring Reference

Technical Reference Guide · Updated June 2026 · 10 min read

When replacing an aging CRT display on a CNC machine with a modern LCD module, understanding the TTL RGB signal pinout is critical. Each CNC manufacturer uses a different connector and signal arrangement. This guide provides the complete pin mapping for the three most common protocols: FANUC Honda MR-20M, Mitsubishi 20/26-pin, and Siemens DB-25.

Key takeaway: Kongto plug-and-play LCD modules match the original pinout exactly — no rewiring, no adapter boards, no CNC parameter changes needed. This reference is provided for engineers who need to understand the signal path or are working with custom integrations.

1. What is TTL RGB?

TTL RGB is a digital video signaling standard. Unlike VGA which uses analog 0-0.7V signals, TTL RGB operates at 0-5V logic levels (TTL = Transistor-Transistor Logic). Key differences from VGA:

ParameterTTL RGB (CNC CRT)VGA (Standard Monitor)
Video Signal Level0-5V Digital (On/Off per channel)0-0.7V Analog (Continuous)
Number of Colors8 (3-bit: 2³ R/G/B)16.7M (8-bit per channel)
Sync SignalSeparate H & V (TTL 0-5V)Separate or Composite H/V
Horizontal Freq15.75 kHz (typically)31-80 kHz
ConnectorVaries by brand (see below)DE-15 (HD-15)
⚠ Important: TTL RGB operates at 5V — connecting it directly to a standard VGA monitor input (which expects 0.7V) can damage the monitor's input circuits. Use a TTL-to-VGA converter or a dedicated CNC LCD replacement module.

2. FANUC — Honda MR-20M 20-Pin Connector

FANUC CRT displays (A61L series, MDT947B, D9MM-11A, D14CM-01A) use the Honda MR-20M 20-pin round connector. This is the most common interface in CNC CRT displays, found on FANUC 0, 0i, 16i, 18i, and 21i series controls.

5. TTL to VGA Conversion

If you need to convert TTL RGB to standard VGA (e.g., for testing or using a standard monitor), the signal path requires:

  1. Level shifting: TTL 5V signals must be attenuated to VGA 0.7V using resistor dividers or a dedicated converter IC
  2. Sync adaptation: TTL sync (15.75 kHz) may need frequency doubling for modern VGA monitors that expect 31 kHz+
  3. Connector conversion: Honda MR-20M or DB-25 to DE-15 (HD-15 VGA connector)

Kongto offers drop-in LCD replacement modules that bypass the need for conversion entirely — they accept TTL RGB directly and display at 800x600 or 1024x768 resolution. No level shifting or sync conversion needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the voltage level of CNC TTL RGB signals?
A: Standard TTL levels are 0V (logic low) to +5V (logic high). This is consistent across FANUC, Mitsubishi, and Siemens CNC CRT interfaces. Some older systems may use +3.3V logic on newer revisions.
Q: Can I test my CNC CRT signals with a multimeter?
A: Yes. With the CNC powered on and running, measure DC voltage at the signal pins. H/V sync pins typically show 2-3V DC average (pulsing). Video pins show 0-5V depending on displayed content. A 5V DC reading on a video pin means that color channel is at full intensity.
Q: What if my CNC display has a different connector than listed here?
A: Some OEMs used custom connectors. Send a clear photo of your CRT display label and connector to info@cncdisplay.com — our engineering team will identify the pinout and confirm compatibility within 24 hours.
