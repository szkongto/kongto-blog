Heidenhain BC120 vs BC110 — What's the Difference?

The BC120 is used primarily on TNC 310/320 controls, while the BC110 is found on TNC 355/360 systems. Both use a 9-inch monochrome CRT with IDC 10-pin connector, but the mounting bracket and CRT yoke impedance differ. Our BC120 kit includes the correct mounting adapter. See BC110 kit →
