Common CNC CRT Display Problems

After 15-30 years of operation, CRTs develop characteristic failure modes:

  • Blank screen / no display — Heater or flyback transformer failure. CNC still operates but screen stays dark.
  • Dim display / low brightness — Phosphor degradation from extended use.
  • Flickering or shaky image — Sync circuit components drift out of spec.
  • Horizontal/vertical line collapse — Deflection circuit failure.
  • Burned-in image / ghosting — Static elements permanently visible on phosphor.

The CNC control itself is typically still functional — replacing just the display with our LCD module restores full visibility.
