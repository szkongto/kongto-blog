About the Delem DM-54 CRT Display

The Delem DM-54 is a 10-inch monochrome CRT used in DA-54/DA-56 press brake controls. After 15-20 years of operation, CRT failure is common — typically flyback transformer burnout or phosphor degradation. This 12.1-inch TFT-LCD kit is a direct replacement with higher brightness, lower power, and permanent reliability.

Compatible Delem Control Models

  • Delem DA-54 (DM-54 CRT)
  • Delem DA-56 (DM-54 CRT)
  • Delem DA-66 (compatible — verify connector)
  • Delem DA-69 (compatible — verify connector)
