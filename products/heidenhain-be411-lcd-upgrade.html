Product Description

The Heidenhain BE411 is a 9-inch monochrome CRT display commonly found on older Heidenhain TNC controllers including the TNC 155, 355, 415, and 425 series. As these CRTs age, they develop symptoms such as dim display, flickering, image distortion, and eventual failure. Our LCD replacement module offers a reliable, long-lasting upgrade.
