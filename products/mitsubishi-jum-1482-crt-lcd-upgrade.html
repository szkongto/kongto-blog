What the JUM-1482 Harness Looks Like

Pull the monitor out and look at the cable end before you order. The JUM-1482 harness carries RGB video on a 9-pin D-sub shell, and the power side ends in a round DIN plug. Both connectors are part of the original chassis wiring. The new module takes the 9-pin D-sub as it is. Send a photo of the DIN plug together with the machine model, and we confirm the power arrangement for that unit before shipping.

Failure Symptoms This Upgrade Ends

  • Dim picture that needs the shop lights switched off to read
  • Colour drift and poor convergence between the three guns
  • Vertical or horizontal jitter from a tired flyback transformer
  • Black screen after the high-voltage section fails

The LCD removes the flyback, the anode lead and the 25 kV section altogether, so those failure modes cannot come back.

JUM-1482 or EUM-1491A? Same Panel, Different Chassis

Both monitors take the same 12.1-inch replacement module, but the two chassis are wired differently. The EUM-1491A keeps its signal and power connectors on the rear panel next to the AC inlet, while the JUM-1482 brings the harness out to a 9-pin D-sub shell plus a round DIN plug. If your cable ends in the rear-panel arrangement, order from the EUM-1491A LCD replacement page instead.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know my monitor is a JUM-1482?
The JUM-1482 number is marked on the model label of the monitor housing, and the same number is printed on the label wrapped around the cable harness. Send a photo of either label if the print is unreadable.
The harness has a 9-pin D-sub and a round DIN plug. What happens to each?
RGB video runs through the 9-pin D-sub, which the LCD accepts directly. The round DIN connector belongs to the power side. Send a photo of the DIN plug with your machine model before ordering so we confirm the power arrangement for your unit.
Do I need to change CNC parameters after fitting the LCD?
No. The module reads the Mitsubishi 9-pin RGB/TTL signal and drives the panel natively, so machine parameters, programs and settings stay untouched.
The panel is smaller than the 14-inch CRT. How does it fit?
The mounting adapter bracket fills the difference between the 12.1-inch panel and the original bezel opening, and bolts into the existing cutout.
How fast does it ship and what is the warranty?
