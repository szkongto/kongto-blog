The Heidenhain BE211 and its variant BE211B are 9-inch monochrome CRT displays used across various Heidenhain TNC CNC controllers. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the difference between BE211 and BE211B?
The BE211B is a minor revision of the BE211 with the same physical dimensions, connector, and video signal. Our LCD module is compatible with both variants.

Q: Does this work with TNC 426/430?
The BE211 was primarily used with earlier TNC models. For TNC 426/430, please check our compatibility matrix or contact us.
