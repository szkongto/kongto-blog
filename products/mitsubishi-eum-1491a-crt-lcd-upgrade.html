EUM-1491A or JUM-1482? Check the Cable End

Both model numbers appear on Mitsubishi 14-inch color monitors, and the replacement module is the same, but the chassis wiring differs. On the EUM-1491A the signal and power connectors sit on the rear panel of the monitor, under the metal cover, next to the AC inlet. On the JUM-1482 the harness ends in a 9-pin D-sub shell together with a round DIN power plug. Look at the cable that leaves your monitor before ordering. If it is the JUM-1482 harness, use the JUM-1482 LCD replacement page.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I confirm that my monitor is an EUM-1491A?
Read the rating label on the rear of the CRT housing. The EUM-1491A label prints MODEL NO. EUM-1491A together with the AC voltage rating. If the print is worn, send a photo of the label to info@cncdisplay.com.
My label reads 220-240 V AC. How is the LCD powered?
That rating belongs to the original CRT. The replacement module runs on DC 24 V at about 12 W. Send a photo of the power connector and your machine model before ordering — we confirm the power arrangement for your chassis before the unit ships.
Is the original 9-pin signal cable reused?
Yes. The LCD accepts the EUM-1491A 9-pin D-Sub RGB/TTL cable directly. No converter, no video scaler, no CNC parameter changes.
Will the image fill the same screen area?
The panel is 12.1 inches against the 14-inch CRT, so the bezel opening is larger than the panel. The mounting adapter bracket covers the gap and holds the panel in the original cutout.
