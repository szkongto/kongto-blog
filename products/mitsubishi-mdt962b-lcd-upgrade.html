Common Mitsubishi MDT962B CRT Display Failures

The MDT962B is a 9-inch monochrome CRT used on Mitsubishi Meldas CNC systems (M50/M60/M300/M500/M600). After decades of use, these units fail in predictable ways:

  • Blank screen / no raster — The CRT flyback transformer fails. The CNC powers up but the display stays dark.
  • Dim display — CRT phosphor degradation. The image is too faint to read under normal shop lighting.
  • Shrinking or rolling image — Dried-out electrolytic capacitors in the deflection driver cause the image to collapse or roll.
  • Intermittent display — Cold solder joints cause the display to cut in and out when the machine is moved or vibrated.

These are CRT hardware failures. The Mitsubishi CNC control is typically still generating the correct video — our LCD displays it immediately after installation.

Why is my Fanuc A61L-0001-0093 CRT monitor blinking or flickering, and how do I fix it?

A blinking or flickering Fanuc CRT is typically caused by aging high-voltage flyback transformers inside the original 9-inch CRT display. The most reliable fix is to replace the obsolete CRT with a plug-and-play LCD upgrade. Can I use a standard consumer LCD monitor to replace my Fanuc CNC CRT display?

No. Standard consumer LCD monitors cannot recognize the low-frequency RGB analog signals (MDA/CGA/EGA timing) that legacy Fanuc CNC systems output. They will display an "Unsupported Signal" error. Consumer monitors also lack the 20-pin Honda connector and cannot withstand shop-floor vibrations, oil mist, and high temperatures. You need an industrial-grade CRT-to-LCD retrofit kit with proprietary timing firmware and original connector compatibility.
