Common CRT Failure Symptoms

  • Color distortion / missing colors — CRT phosphor degradation, most common in color CRTs after 10,000+ hours
  • Dim display — Electron gun wear reduces brightness to unreadable levels
  • Screen flicker / image jitter — Flyback transformer or capacitor degradation
  • Focus blur / convergence error — Aging CRT yoke or deflection board
  • No display / blank screen — High-voltage failure or CRT heater burnout

Our LCD module eliminates all CRT-related failure modes permanently. 70% lower power, no high voltage, no flicker.
