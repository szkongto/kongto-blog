Common CRT Failure Symptoms

  • Blank screen / no power — Flyback transformer failure, the most common CRT failure mode
  • Dim or unreadable display — CRT phosphor wear after extended operation (15,000+ hours)
  • Flickering or image distortion — Capacitor degradation in horizontal/vertical sync circuits
  • Color shift or missing colors — Aging electron gun or video amplifier circuit failure

Our LCD module eliminates all CRT-related failure modes permanently. Plug-and-play replacement, no rewiring or parameter changes needed.
