Common CRT Failure Symptoms

  • Blank screen / no power — Flyback transformer failure, most common
  • Dim or unreadable display — CRT phosphor wear after 15,000+ hours
  • Flickering or image distortion — Capacitor degradation in sync circuits
  • Color shift or missing colors — Aging electron gun or video amplifier
