Common FANUC A61L 0001 0092 CRT Display Failures

The FANUC A61L 0001 0092 is a 9-inch monochrome CRT on FANUC Series 6 milling and turning controls.

  • Blank screen / no display — CRT heater burnout or flyback transformer failure. The CNC control still operates but the screen stays dark.
  • Dim display — unreadable in shop lighting — CRT phosphor degradation after decades of continuous use.
  • Flickering / unstable image — Electrolytic capacitors in the deflection circuit dried out, causing the image to shake or roll.
  • Burn-in / ghost image — Permanent phosphor wear from static screen elements (position readout, status bars).

All CRT hardware failures. The FANUC control itself is typically functional — our LCD module restores display without control modifications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will this LCD work with my older FANUC control?

Yes. This replacement is specifically designed for machines using this model CRT. The LCD reproduces the same video timing as the original monochrome CRT — no parameter changes, no adapter cables, no signal converters needed.

The CRT is completely dead. How do I know the CNC control is still working?

Listen for the spindle relay, watch for IO activity LEDs on the control board, or try MDI operation blindly. In most cases, a dead CRT means the display has failed — the control is still generating video. Our LCD will confirm this immediately after installation.

Installation difficulty?

10–15 minutes. Power off, remove old CRT, mount LCD, reconnect keyed connectors, power on. No soldering, no parameter changes.
