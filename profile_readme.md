# Kongto Technology | Industrial CNC Display Solutions

## About

**Kongto Technology (深圳市江图科技有限公司)** specializes in industrial video display solutions for CNC systems worldwide. Founded in 2013, we provide CRT-to-LCD upgrade modules, video signal converters, and custom industrial displays for FANUC, Mitsubishi, Siemens, Mazak, Okuma, and Haas CNC controllers.
