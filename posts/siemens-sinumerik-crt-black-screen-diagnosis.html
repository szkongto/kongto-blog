Siemens SINUMERIK CRT Black Screen — Complete Diagnosis & Fix Guide

SINUMERIK CRT black screen is the most common display failure on Siemens CNC controls from the 810, 820, and early 840 series. Unlike generic monitor failures, Siemens SINUMERIK displays are tightly integrated with the NCK (Numerical Control Kernel) — a blank screen can mean a CRT hardware fault, a CNC system fault, or a power supply issue.

This guide covers four diagnostic methods ranked by speed, using only the tools already on your shop floor: the monitor LED on the 03840 board, a multimeter, and your ears. No scope, no schematic, no Siemens service contract required.

High-voltage warning: Siemens SINUMERIK CRT monitors operate at 15-28 kV anode voltage. The CRT can retain lethal charge even after power-off. Always discharge through a grounded resistor before handling. If not trained in HV safety, have a qualified technician perform the diagnosis.

Step 1: Read the Monitor LED (Fastest Diagnosis — 30 seconds)

Every SINUMERIK 810/820 system has a two-color (red/green) LED on the 03840 CPU board. This LED is your first and fastest diagnostic tool — it directly indicates the fault domain without opening the CRT.

Step 2: Measure the Backup Battery

The SINUMERIK 810/820 NCK uses a 3.6V lithium backup battery (Siemens part 6FC5247 or equivalent) to maintain SRAM-stored NC and PLC data when the main power is off.

Test procedure:

  1. Locate the battery holder on the 03840/03850 board
  2. Measure voltage at the battery terminals with a multimeter
  3. A healthy battery reads 3.4-3.6V; replace if below 3.0V
  4. After battery replacement, reload NCK and PLC data from backup

Critical: Replacing the battery without backup data will leave the control in an uninitialized state. Always dump NC and PLC data to a PC or external storage before battery removal. The standard SINUMERIK 810 boot sequence requires loading TEA1, TEA2, and PCP data after battery replacement.

Step 3: Check the 24V DC Power Supply

The SINUMERIK 810 system operates on 24Vdc ±10%. The CRT itself uses an internal DC-AC inverter to generate the 15-28 kV anode voltage, fed from the 24V rail.

Measurement points:

  • Measure at terminal X111 on the 03840 board — should read 24V ± 10%
  • Check under load — voltage dropping below 21V during operation indicates PSU overload or failing rectifier
  • Common 24V rail issues: loose terminal screws (vibration loosens them over years), failing electrolytic capacitors in the PSU, or external short circuits (e.g., axis brake coil short to ground)

Pro tip: If the CRT display works when the machine is cold but blanks after 10-30 minutes, suspect a thermal failure in the 24V PSU or the CRT flyback transformer. The flyback transformer develops internal arcing as temperature rises, eventually triggering HV shutdown.

Step 4: Listen and Test the CRT Itself

A functioning CRT produces an audible 15.75 kHz horizontal sweep whine. This sound is the single best indicator of CRT HV section health.

Frequently Asked Questions

My SINUMERIK 810M screen is black but the machine runs — what does that mean?
The CNC control is operational but the display path is broken. Check the 03840 monitor LED pattern first. If the LED is normal but the CRT is dark, the CRT itself has failed. An LCD replacement restores display without affecting machine operation.
Will an LCD replacement work with the Siemens IDC 16-pin connector?
Yes. Kongto LCD kits include a driver board that reads the SINUMERIK RGB TTL video signal directly from the IDC 16-pin connector. No signal converter or wiring adapter needed — it is a direct electrical and mechanical replacement.
Do I need to update Siemens firmware for LCD?
No. The LCD driver board emulates the original CRT signal timing. The SINUMERIK control operates exactly as before. No software changes, no parameter updates, no PLC program modifications.
What causes the 4 Hz flash on 03840 board?
Almost always the 3.6V backup battery. Replace the battery, reload NC/PLC data from backup, and the system should boot normally. If the CRT is still dark after boot, the CRT hardware has failed during the downtime — proceed with LCD replacement.
I see a thin horizontal line instead of a display — what is this?
This is vertical deflection failure — the CRT field deflection IC or yoke coil has failed. Common on aging SINUMERIK CRTs. The CRT can only produce a single scan line. This is not repairable economically. LCD replacement is the recommended fix.
