Series Overview

The Custom Industrial Display Series from Kongto Technology is developed specifically for special installation space requirements, breaking through standard size limitations to provide customized display solutions for legacy equipment upgrades, special workstation layouts, and compact machinery applications.

Each model in the series is engineered to replace aging CRT displays or fill non-standard cutout dimensions that off-the-shelf industrial monitors cannot accommodate. The displays support multiple video input formats, operate across a wide temperature range, and are built to withstand the vibration, dust, and humidity common in factory floor environments.

Customization Process

Kongto Technology follows a structured five-step customization workflow to ensure every display meets your exact specifications. From initial consultation to final delivery, the entire process typically completes within 15-25 working days depending on complexity.

  1. Requirement Discussion: Provide installation space dimensions (cutout size, depth clearance), required signal interfaces (VGA, HDMI, DVI, etc.), protection requirements (IP rating), operating temperature range, and any special considerations such as touch input or high-brightness outdoor viewing. Share photos or drawings of the existing equipment cutout —this helps our engineers identify the optimal panel size and mounting solution on the first pass.
  2. Solution Design: Our engineering team reviews your requirements and prepares a detailed custom solution including a 3D CAD drawing of the proposed display with dimensions, interface locations, and mounting bracket design. A formal quotation with itemized costs for customization, tooling (if applicable), and MOQ is provided for your review.
  3. Sample Confirmation: Once the design is approved, a prototype sample is manufactured and tested for fit, function, signal compatibility, and environmental tolerance. The sample is shipped to you for physical fit-check and functional validation. Any adjustments to the design are made at this stage before proceeding to production.
  4. Mass Production: After sample approval, batch production begins. Each unit undergoes a 24-hour burn-in test and full functional check (input signal test, display uniformity, temperature cycling) before packaging. Production typically completes within 10-15 working days for standard customization.
  5. After-Sales Support: Every custom display comes with a 12-month warranty covering manufacturing defects. Our technical support team is available for installation guidance, troubleshooting, and firmware updates. Replacement units are expedited for any warranty claims to minimize your production downtime.
