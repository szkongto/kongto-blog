1. Overview

The FANUC A61L-0001-0094 is a widely deployed 14-inch color CRT display in legacy FANUC 0i, 16i, 18i and 21i series CNC systems. After years of industrial operation these CRT units develop brightness decay, color shift, flicker, focus blur and eventually complete failure. This guide documents a complete retrofit that replaces the obsolete color CRT with an industrial-grade TFT-LCD module — a direct, plug-and-play swap with no modification to the CNC control.

2.2 Common Aging Failures

  • Progressive Brightness Reduction: CRT phosphor and cathode aging cause continuous brightness decay, poor daytime visibility and operator eye strain.
  • Color Cast and Permanent Color Shift: RGB gun aging imbalance drifts color temperature (red looks orange, blue looks purple), which hides G-code syntax highlighting and alarm colors.
  • Intermittent Flicker and Geometric Distortion: deflection coil and focus-circuit aging cause jitter, pincushion and trapezoid distortion, making program text hard to read.
  • High-Voltage Insulation Degradation: years of 10-15kV operation age the flyback and socket insulation, risking arcing, high-voltage leakage and fire hazard.
  • Complete Black-Screen Failure: cathode filament burnout or flyback failure disables the display entirely, forcing unplanned machine downtime.
  • Obsolete Parts Supply: the CRT and its components are long discontinued; used pull-outs are scarce, costly and near end-of-life, so repairs get slower and pricier.

5. Standard Installation Procedure (30-40 Minutes)

The KTV148 LCD module is mechanically and electrically compatible with the original A61L-0001-0094 CRT. No CNC parameter changes are needed. Expect 30-40 minutes for a careful first install.

5.1 Safety Preparation

  1. Shut down and disconnect the CNC main power. Ensure complete power isolation.
  2. Allow 8-10 minutes for internal capacitors to discharge before touching any connectors.
  3. Use an anti-static wrist strap to protect the LCD module electronics.
  4. Photograph the existing wiring and CRT orientation before disconnecting, for reference during reinstallation.

5.2 Original CRT Removal

  1. Disconnect the 20-pin Honda signal cable from the rear of the CRT (press the release latch, pull gently).
  2. Disconnect the DC 24V power cable from the CRT power connector.
  3. Remove the mounting screws securing the CRT bezel to the control cabinet.
  4. Lift the CRT assembly out with both hands — it is heavy and fragile, so never carry it by the signal cable or connector neck.

5.3 LCD Module Installation

  1. Unpack the KTV148 module and inspect for shipping damage. Confirm the model label targets A61L-0001-0094 compatibility.
  2. Position the module into the original CRT cut-out. It uses the factory mounting points.
  3. Align the mounting holes — the module is keyed and cannot be installed upside down. Seat it flush against the cabinet panel.
  4. Secure with the original screws. Snug is enough; overtightening distorts the front panel and affects uniformity.

5.4 Cable Connection

  1. Connect the original 20-pin Honda signal cable to the module. The connector is keyed — if it will not slide in, do not force it; reverse and retry.
  2. Connect the original DC 24V power cable to the module. It runs straight from the machine supply; no adapter or converter is needed.
  3. Confirm each connector is fully seated and any latch is engaged. Gently tug each cable to verify.

5.5 Power-On Test and Calibration

  1. Restore main power and start the CNC with the normal procedure.
  2. The module shows the CNC boot screen automatically — no mode selection is required.
  3. Check sharpness: text should be crisp across the full screen.
  4. Check color: G-code highlighting and alarm messages (red/yellow) should look correct.
  5. If needed, adjust brightness/contrast and screen position through the OSD (front-panel buttons). The auto-adjust function normally fixes positioning in under 10 seconds.
  6. Run the machine at least 30 minutes to confirm thermal stability and a stable image.

Important Notes:

  • This retrofit needs no CNC parameter changes. The module is electrically and mechanically transparent to the control.
  • Do not open the old CRT housing. It stores a dangerous high-voltage charge for hours after power-off.
  • If the module shows nothing after install, reseat the 20-pin Honda connector first, then verify 24V DC at the power connector.

7. Operation and Maintenance Guidelines

7.1 Periodic Inspection (Every 6 Months)

  • Inspect the 20-pin Honda cable for looseness, oxidation or bent pins; reseat if needed.
  • Check the DC 24V connection and measure voltage at the connector (21-27V DC under load).
  • Clean the front panel with a soft, lint-free cloth lightly dampened with 70%+ isopropyl alcohol. Do not use ammonia cleaners — they strip the anti-glare coating.
  • Confirm the OSD still opens and brightness/contrast have not drifted.

7.2 Long-Term Storage and Backup

  • If the original CRT still works, keep it as a temporary backup in a dry room (15-25°C, below 60% RH).
  • Spare KTV148 modules store without degradation for years; rotate stock if you keep several.
