FANUC A61L-0001-0092 MDT947B 9-inch Monochrome LCD Retrofit Guide

2026-05-03 FANUC CNC Display Solutions

Product Overview

The FANUC A61L-0001-0092 (MDT947B) is a 9-inch color CRT display originally used in FANUC CNC systems. As these CRT displays age, they suffer from brightness loss, flickering, and eventual failure. Our LCD retrofit kit provides a cost-effective, reliable solution with modern LCD technology while maintaining 100% compatibility.

FANUC A61L-0001-0092 (MDT947B) LCD Display —Front View | Source: Kongto Technology

Key Specifications

  • Model: A61L-0001-0092 (MDT947B)
  • Type: 9-inch monochrome LCD (replaces CRT)
  • Interface: HONDA 20-pin signal connector
  • Power: DC 24V (compatible with original CRT power supply)
  • Compatibility: FANUC 0i/16i/18i series CNC systems

Why Retrofit?

Color CRT displays have a typical lifespan of 15-20 years. Common issues include reduced brightness, flickering, and difficulty obtaining replacement parts. LCD technology solves these problems with a lifespan of 50,000+ hours, significantly reducing maintenance costs and downtime.

CRT vs. LCD Display Comparison —Significant improvement in clarity and reliability | Source: Kongto Technology

Installation Guide

Our retrofit kit is designed for plug-and-play installation:

  1. Power down the CNC system completely
  2. Disconnect the old CRT cables and remove the CRT unit
  3. Install the LCD panel into the original mounting frame
  4. Connect the signal adapter cable and power supply
  5. Power on and verify the display works correctly
