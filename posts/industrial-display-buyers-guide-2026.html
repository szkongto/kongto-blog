Industrial Display Buyer's Guide 2026: CNC CRT to LCD Replacement

1. Market Overview

The CNC display screen market is valued at $1.88 billion in 2026, growing at a 12.22% CAGR toward $3.84 billion by 2032. Three forces drive this:

  • CRT phase-out: The youngest CNC CRTs are 20+ years old. Flyback transformers and CRTs are effectively extinct in new-manufacture supply.
  • EU Machinery Regulation 2023/1230: Effective Jan 2027, triggering pre-compliance upgrades. German SMEs added networked displays at record rates in 2025-2026.
  • Smart-factory retrofits: Siemens reported 22% YoY growth in retrofit monitor revenue, with a 3:1 ratio favoring retrofits over greenfield lines.

5. Decision Framework

Buy LCD retrofit when: CRT has failed, machine runs production daily, planned use 1+ years, or operators report eye strain.

Consider used CRT only when: Machine retiring within 6 months, or stopgap while LCD kit ships.

Do not buy OEM replacement CRTs: Refurbished old units at 3-5x the cost of a new LCD.

6. Market Outlook

CNC display market growing at 12.22% CAGR. LCD holds 61.21% of industrial monitor market. Retrofits outnumber greenfield installations at major CNC builders. 2026 is the best window to transition from CRT to LCD.
