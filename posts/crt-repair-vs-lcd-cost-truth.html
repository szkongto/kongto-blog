CRT Repair vs LCD Upgrade: The 5-Year Cost Truth

Bottom line: A FANUC 0-TC CRT needed 4 repairs in 18 months totaling $1,850. An LCD retrofit costs $179-600 once, lasting 50,000+ hours. Based on Kongto Technology service records and published case studies.

CRT Failure Breakdown (500+ service records)

  • Flyback transformer failure: ~40%
  • Capacitor aging: ~25%
  • CRT tube wear/dimming: ~20%
  • Deflection circuit: ~10%
  • Signal connector: ~5%
