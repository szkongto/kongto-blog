Breaking Down the Real Costs

When a CNC CRT fails, shop managers face three options: repair, source a used OEM CRT, or upgrade to LCD. Each path has very different cost profiles.

OptionUpfront Cost5-Year CostDowntime
CRT repair-600,000-3,0003-7 days
Used OEM CRT-1,500-1,5001-2 weeks
LCD upgrade-550-55015-30 min

The LCD upgrade pays for itself the moment it avoids the first CRT repair. Each repair cycle costs more in downtime than the repair itself.

Hidden Cost: Dim-Screen Operator Errors

A dim CRT forces operators to lean in and guess at readouts. A single misread tool offset can scrap a ,000 workpiece. Shops that have switched to LCD report zero "I couldn't read the screen" errors.
