Key Selection Criteria

  • Input compatibility: Does it accept RGB/TTL, CGA, EGA, or VGA? Know your CNC output.
  • Latency: For CNC, under 1 frame is essential. Generic gaming converters add 2-3 frames of lag.
  • Integration: Built-in converters (inside LCD housing) beat external boxes that need separate power and mounting.
  • Power: DC 24V from the CNC rail is preferred over separate AC adapters.

Our KTV series LCD modules have the converter built in — no external box, no separate power, no extra cables.
