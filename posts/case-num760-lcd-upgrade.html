NUM 760 CNC CRT to LCD Custom Replacement — Shanghai Chuanqi Case Study

The NUM 760 is a French-designed CNC control manufactured by NUM (Schneider Electric), widely used in machine tools across Europe and Asia. Its original 9-inch monochrome CRT (model MVB 241 NU) is a known failure point — after 15-20 years, the CRT tube dims, flickers, or fails completely. OEM replacements are long discontinued. This case study documents a successful custom LCD replacement for a NUM 760 control at Shanghai Chuanqi Mechanical & Electrical Equipment Co., Ltd.

NUM 760 CNC original 9-inch monochrome CRT — model MVB 241 NU | Source: Kongto Technology

The Challenge

The customer's NUM 760 CRT had become so dim that the operator could no longer read tool offset values. The 9-inch monochrome display was original to the machine — over 15 years old — and replacement CRTs were completely unavailable. A full control upgrade was prohibitively expensive. The customer needed a practical LCD replacement that preserved the existing control and required no parameter changes.

NUM 760 CRT removal — disconnecting the original display | Source: Kongto Technology

NUM 760 CRT disconnected — preparing for LCD installation | Source: Kongto Technology

The Solution

We engineered a custom 9-inch TFT LCD module specifically for the NUM 760's MVB 241 NU display interface. The module reads the NUM's monochrome video signal directly — no external converter or frame grabber required. The LCD mounts in the original CRT housing using the same screw holes, preserving the factory appearance. DC 24V power is drawn from the same rail the CRT used. The entire upgrade is plug-and-play and completely reversible.

Results

  • Complete CRT-to-LCD replacement for NUM 760 CNC control
  • 300% brightness improvement over the original CRT
  • No CNC parameter changes required
  • Zero modifications to the control cabinet
  • LED backlight rated for 50,000+ hours
  • Fully reversible — original CRT can be reinstalled
