Delem DM-54 CRT to LCD Custom Replacement — Case Study

The Delem DM-54 is a dedicated CNC controller for press brake bending machines, widely used in sheet metal fabrication. Its original 10-inch CRT display is prone to dimming, flickering, and eventual failure — and OEM replacements are long discontinued. This case study documents a successful custom CRT-to-LCD replacement using a 12.1-inch TFT LCD with a mounting adapter plate.

The Challenge

The customer's Delem DM-54 controller had a failing CRT — image was dim, colors were shifting, and the machine was becoming difficult to operate accurately. The original CRT uses a proprietary signal interface and non-standard mounting hole pattern, ruling out off-the-shelf LCD monitors. A full controller replacement would cost ,000+ with significant downtime. The customer needed a drop-in LCD replacement that preserved the original controls and required zero parameter changes.

Delem DM-54 CRT front view — failing display with dim image | Source: Kongto Technology

The Solution

We provided a custom 12.1-inch TFT LCD replacement module with the following specifications: 800×600 resolution (SVGA), 400 cd/m² brightness, DC 24V input, and a custom signal adapter that reads the Delem's RGB video output directly. A bespoke mounting adapter plate was fabricated to bridge the original CRT bezel opening to the smaller LCD panel, ensuring a professional flush fit. The entire assembly is a bolt-on replacement — no panel modifications, no wiring changes, no software configuration.

KTV148 LCD module with custom signal adapter for Delem DM-54 | Source: Kongto Technology

Installation & Results

Installation was completed on-site in under 30 minutes. The old CRT was removed, the custom LCD module was mounted using the adapter plate, and the existing signal and power cables were reconnected. Upon power-up, the Delem DM-54 displayed the control interface immediately — no parameter changes, no dip-switch adjustments, no learning curve. The image was significantly brighter, sharper, and flicker-free. The customer reported that operators could read the screen from across the shop floor for the first time in years.

Key Takeaways

  • Custom adapter plate enabled perfect fit — 12.1" LCD in a 10" CRT cutout
  • Signal compatibility achieved without any controller modifications
  • Total on-site installation time: under 30 minutes
  • Brightness improved ~100%, zero flicker, no geometric distortion
  • Cost: fraction of a full controller replacement (,000+)
