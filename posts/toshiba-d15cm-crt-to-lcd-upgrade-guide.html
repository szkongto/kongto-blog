TOSHIBA D15CM 14-inch CRT to LCD Upgrade Guide

Published: September 11, 2026 | Category: Toshiba LCD Retrofit | Author: szkongto01

The TOSHIBA D15CM is a 14-inch colour CRT display module. Toshiba built CRT displays for CNC control manufacturers as an OEM supplier rather than selling them under its own control brand, which is why a D15CM often turns up in a machine whose control badge says something else entirely — FANUC, Mazak or a machine builder's own name.

That OEM relationship is also why Toshiba part numbers are so easily mixed up. The D9MM-11A and D14CM-01A are separate Toshiba models that correspond to FANUC A61L part numbers and use the 20-pin Honda MR-20M connector. The D15CM is a different 14-inch colour unit with its own listing. Confirm the marking on the display before ordering.

⚠️ Quick Answer: A TOSHIBA D15CM 14-inch colour CRT is replaced with a 12.1-inch TFT-LCD industrial panel, 800x600, 400 cd/m2. It runs on the original AC 100-240V supply and the original RGB analog signal path, so no rewiring and no parameter changes. $480, outright sale, 2-year warranty.

Installation Steps

1. Isolate and Discharge

Switch off the control and disconnect the machine from mains. The CRT holds high voltage after shutdown — allow one minute before opening the housing.

2. Record Label and Connector

Photograph the D15CM label, the connector and the cable routing. Send the photos if you want the fit confirmed before you start.

3. Remove the CRT

Undo the mounting screws and draw the 14-inch CRT out of the bezel. Disconnect the signal cable and the AC supply. No soldering at any stage.

4. Mount the Module

Fit the 12.1-inch LCD into the original frame so it sits behind the existing bezel opening. Reuse the original fasteners.

5. Reconnect and Power Up

Reconnect the signal cable and AC 100-240V supply, then start the control. The picture appears with no parameter changes; set brightness on the module.

Expect 10 to 15 minutes of work. Because the module reuses the original AC supply and the original RGB analog signal, the machine is back in production in the same maintenance window.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: My machine is a FANUC but the display says TOSHIBA D15CM. Is that normal?
A: Yes. Toshiba supplied CRT displays to CNC control manufacturers as an OEM, so the display brand and the control brand frequently differ. Order against the display marking.
Q: Is the D15CM the same as the D14CM-01A?
A: No. The D14CM-01A and D9MM-11A correspond to FANUC A61L part numbers and use the 20-pin Honda MR-20M connector. The D15CM is a separate 14-inch colour unit — send a photo of the label and connector and we will match it.
Q: Does the module need a DC power supply?
A: No. The D15CM is an AC mains display rated 100-240V, and the replacement is compatible with that same AC input.
Q: How long does the LCD last compared with the CRT?
A: The CRT is rated around 15,000 hours. The LCD module is rated 50,000+ hours and has no tube, no flyback transformer and no high-voltage section to age.
Q: Can you supply a replacement if my connector turns out to be unusual?
A: Yes — we are the factory, so cable length and connector arrangement can be built to match. Send photos of the label and connector and we will confirm within four working hours.
