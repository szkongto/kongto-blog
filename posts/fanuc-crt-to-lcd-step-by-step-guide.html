How to Replace a Fanuc CRT with LCD: Complete Step-by-Step Guide

Applies to: Fanuc A61L-0001 series (0072-0097) | D9MM-11A | MDT947B | Install time: 10-15 minutes | Tools: Phillips screwdriver only

Can I Directly Swap the Fanuc CRT with an LCD?

Yes — 100% plug-and-play. Our LCD replacement module uses the exact same 20-pin Honda connector (MR-20L) and mounting hole pattern as the original CRT. Unplug the old CRT, plug in the new LCD. No wiring changes, no signal converters, no CNC parameter modifications. The LCD auto-syncs to Fanuc video signal timing.

Step 1: Power Down and Safety

Turn off main power. Wait 3 minutes for capacitor discharge — CRT supplies hold dangerous voltages. Wear a grounding strap.

Step 2: Remove the Old CRT

  1. Remove 4 mounting screws securing the CRT housing
  2. Pull CRT forward to access rear connectors
  3. Disconnect 20-pin Honda signal cable (press locking tab, pull straight)
  4. Disconnect DC 24V power wires — note wire colors

Step 3: Install the LCD Module

  1. Place LCD into same opening — mounting holes align 100%
  2. Secure with same 4 screws from Step 2
  3. Plug 20-pin Honda cable into LCD — keyed connector prevents incorrect insertion
  4. Connect DC 24V power

Step 4: Power On

Restore power. LCD displays within 5-10 seconds. Use OSD buttons to adjust brightness. No CNC parameter changes needed.

Step-by-Step Replacement Process

1. Safety Lockout

Lock out CNC power at the disconnect. CRTs retain up to 28 kV after power-off. Wait 5 min for bleed, then discharge the anode with a high-voltage probe.

2. Remove Old CRT

Unscrew 4 mounting screws. Disconnect HONDA 20-pin signal and DC 24V power. CRT weighs 2-5 kg — use two hands.

3. Install LCD Module

Position LCD in mounting area. Secure with provided screws. Connect signal cable (keyed connector — one orientation only). Connect DC 24V power.

4. Power On

Remove LOTO, restore power. LCD displays immediately. Use OSD to adjust brightness and position if needed.
