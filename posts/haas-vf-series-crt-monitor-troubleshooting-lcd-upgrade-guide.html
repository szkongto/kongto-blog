4. Installation Guide (10-15 Minutes)

This upgrade requires no special tools, no wiring modifications, and no CNC parameter changes. Here is the complete installation process:

Step 1: Power Down

Turn off the main disconnect switch on the back of the HAAS machine. Wait 2 minutes for internal capacitors to discharge. Confirm the control panel screen is completely dark.

Step 2: Remove CRT

Unscrew the 4 mounting screws on the CRT bezel. Slide the CRT forward carefully. Disconnect the 9-pin D-Sub signal cable and DC 12V power connector from the back of the CRT unit.

Step 3: Install LCD

Place the 12.1-inch LCD module into the original mounting frame. The screw holes align 100% with the original CRT positions. Secure with the original screws. No drilling or adapters needed.

Step 4: Connect Cables

Plug the original 9-pin D-Sub cable into the LCD module signal input. Connect the DC 12V power cable. Both connectors are keyed — they only fit one way, so incorrect insertion is impossible.

Step 5: Power On

Restore main power and start the HAAS machine. The LCD will display the HAAS control interface automatically. Use the OSD buttons on the LCD module to adjust brightness, contrast, and screen position to your preference.
