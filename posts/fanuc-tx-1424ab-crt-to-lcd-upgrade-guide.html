FANUC TX-1424AB 14-inch CRT to LCD Upgrade Guide

Published: September 11, 2026 | Category: FANUC LCD Retrofit | Author: szkongto01

The FANUC TX-1424AB is a 14-inch colour CRT display module. It appears on FANUC controls alongside the A61L-0001 series part numbers, and the two labelling systems describe overlapping hardware: the TX-1424AB label and the A61L-0001-0074 part number both identify a 14-inch colour display module with a 20-pin Honda MR-20M interface and a DC 24V supply.

That matters when you order. If your CRT carries the TX-1424AB label, the module in this guide is the one that fits — there is no need to convert the label to an A61L number first, and no need to send the old unit in for exchange.

⚠️ Quick Answer: A FANUC TX-1424AB 14-inch colour CRT is replaced with a 12.1-inch TFT-LCD industrial panel, 800x600, 400 cd/m2. It reuses the original 20-pin Honda MR-20M connector and the original DC 24V supply, so no rewiring and no parameter changes. $299, outright sale, 2-year warranty.

Installation Steps

1. Isolate the Machine

Switch off the CNC and lock out the supply. The CRT stores high voltage even when the control is off — allow one minute for the capacitors to discharge.

2. Record the Label

Photograph the TX-1424AB label and the cable routing before disassembly. Send us the photo if you want the fit confirmed against your control.

3. Extract the CRT

Remove the bezel and the CRT mounting screws, then slide the unit out. Disconnect the 20-pin Honda MR-20M plug and the DC 24V wires.

4. Mount the 12.1-inch Panel

Fit the LCD module into the supplied frame, which fills the original 14-inch bezel opening. Secure with the original fasteners.

5. Connect and Verify

Reconnect the 20-pin Honda connector and DC 24V, restore power and start the control. Confirm the picture, then set brightness on the module.

Installation runs 10 to 15 minutes and needs no soldering. Because the module keeps the DC 24V rail and the MR-20M interface unchanged, the control sees the same signal it always did and no CNC parameters are touched.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: My CRT says TX-1424AB but my colleague's says A61L-0001-0074 — same part?
A: They identify the same 14-inch colour display family. The modules are configured to their own listings, so order against the label actually printed on your CRT.
Q: Does the replacement need a 110V or 220V supply?
A: Neither. The TX-1424AB module runs from the original DC 24V display supply in the cabinet, exactly as the CRT did.
Q: Will the smaller 12.1-inch panel leave a visible gap?
A: No. The module is supplied with a mounting frame that fills the original 14-inch bezel opening, so the front of the cabinet looks finished, not adapted.
Q: Can the LCD be fitted to a machine that is still in daily production?
A: Yes. The swap takes 10 to 15 minutes and does not disturb programs, offsets or parameters, so it can be scheduled inside a normal maintenance window.
Q: What is supplied with the module?
A: The 12.1-inch LCD module, its mounting frame and the interface cabling needed for the 20-pin Honda MR-20M connection. Contact us if your machine needs a specific cable length.
