2. Video Signal Converters

Kongto Technology video signal converters bridge the gap between legacy CRT video standards (CGA, EGA, RGBS, TTL RGB) and modern VGA input displays. These compact converter units are essential for retrofitting CNC machines and industrial equipment where the original video signal format must be preserved while upgrading to LCD technology. All converters are rated for industrial temperature ranges and feature rugged aluminum housings.

3. Touch Screen Solutions

Kongto Technology offers three touch screen technologies to suit different industrial environments. Resistive touch is ideal for gloved operation and wet environments. Projected capacitive (PCAP) provides multi-touch capability and superior optical clarity for modern HMI applications. Surface acoustic wave (SAW) delivers the highest optical clarity with no additional coating layers, making it suitable for medical and clean-room applications.
