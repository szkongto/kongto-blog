Cost Comparison: Repair vs Replace

Short-term view: A single CRT repair ($80-$200) may appear cheaper than an LCD upgrade ($155-$399). However, 50-70% of repaired CRTs fail again within 12-18 months due to the age of remaining components.

3-year cost projection:

  • CRT repair route: $200 (first repair) + $200 (second repair, 70% probability) + $400 (final tube replacement when parts become unavailable) = ~$800-$900 over 3 years
  • LCD upgrade route: $155-$399 (one-time purchase with 2-year warranty) = $155-$399 total over 3+ years — no ongoing maintenance

Other Factors to Consider

  • Image quality: LCD provides 800×600+ resolution vs 640×400 for CRTs, with 350-450 cd/m² brightness and no geometric distortion
  • Power consumption: LCD uses 70% less power than CRT — savings of $50-$100/year in continuous operation
  • Parts availability: CRT components (flyback transformers, CRT tubes) are increasingly hard to find. LCD parts are readily available globally
  • Installation: LCD upgrade takes 10-15 minutes, no CNC parameter changes needed. Repairs require specialized CRT technician knowledge
