HAAS CRT Monitor Troubleshooting — VF Series Display Fix & LCD Upgrade

Last updated: July 28, 2026 · Reading time: 7 min

HAAS VF-series machines from the 1990s-2000s use a distinctive 9-pin monochrome CRT display. When it goes dark, diagnosing the root cause is straightforward — and LCD replacement is simpler and cheaper than most CRT repairs.

Safety note: HAAS Classic Control CRTs operate at 12V DC internally, but the CRT anode still holds 8-12 kV. Discharge before handling internal components.

Quick Diagnosis Flow

  1. Check 9-pin connector — Most common failure. Remove, inspect pins, clean with contact cleaner. Re-tighten thumbscrews firmly.
  2. Measure pin 2 voltage — Should read +12V DC. Zero volts = blown fuse on I/O board (F2 on most Classic Control boards).
  3. Adjust brightness pot — Front-panel brightness knob. Turn fully clockwise. If no response → CRT tube or flyback failure.
  4. Look for bulging caps — Remove CRT housing. Swollen capacitors on the power board are common after 10-15 years.
  5. Swap test — Connect Kongto LCD kit. If it displays, CRT is confirmed dead.
