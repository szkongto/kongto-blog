FANUC CRT Screen Flickering — Diagnose the Cause & Upgrade to LCD

FANUC CRT flickering is one of the most common failure modes on legacy CNC machines. The screen rolls, shakes, or flashes intermittently — making it impossible to read tool positions, program blocks, or alarms. Over 60% of FANUC 0/0i/16i/18i/21i series displays older than 12 years develop this issue.

This guide walks through a 4-step diagnosis and explains why LCD replacement — not CRT repair — is the cost-effective permanent fix.

CRT high-voltage warning: CRT anode voltages reach 15-28 kV and remain charged after power-off. Always discharge before handling. If unsure, hire a qualified technician.

The 4-Step Diagnosis Routine

Step 1: Observe the flickering pattern

Vertical roll = sync signal path problem. Horizontal shake = power supply capacitors. Full flash = flyback transformer HV. Take a 30-second phone video to identify the pattern.

Step 2: Check the HONDA 20-pin cable

FANUC A61L CRTs use a standard HONDA 20-pin connector. Reseat both ends. Look for bent pins or corrosion. A poor connection is the cheapest fix — but even if it works, CRT wear continues.

Step 3: Listen for audio cues

  • Normal: Faint 15.75 kHz whine — CRT is working
  • Buzzing/sizzling: Flyback transformer arcing — needs replacement soon
  • Ticking/clicking: HV shutdown cycling — immediate replacement needed
  • Silent: No HV — CRT is dead

Step 4: Test with a substitute display

Connect a known-working display to the same CNC control. If it works cleanly, the problem is in your CRT — not the CNC system. A Kongto LCD test unit can confirm this in 5 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does flickering damage my CNC machine?
No — the flickering is in the CRT display only, not the CNC control. Your machine continues running. However, a failing flyback can cause arcing that trips HV shutdown, making the display go black.
Can I use any LCD screen as replacement?
No. FANUC CRTs use specific video timing and signal levels (RGB TTL, 15.75 kHz). A generic LCD needs a signal converter. Kongto kits include matched driver boards pre-configured for FANUC.
