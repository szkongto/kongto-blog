Common 0i Display Failure Symptoms

  • Blank screen, control running: CRT failed. Verify DC 24V at connector.
  • Flickering/rolling: Check HSYNC/VSYNC. Corroded HONDA pins common after 20+ years.
  • Dim display: Phosphor degradation. ~30% original brightness after 10+ years. LCD replacement fixes it.
  • Color shift: CRT gun driver failure. Not repairable economically.
  • Horizontal line: Vertical deflection failed. LCD replacement required.
