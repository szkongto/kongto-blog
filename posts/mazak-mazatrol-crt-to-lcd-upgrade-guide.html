Mazak Mazatrol CRT to LCD Upgrade Guide

Published: June 27, 2026 | Author: szkongto01

Mazak Mazatrol controls power some of the most productive CNC machines ever built. Their CRT displays — including MDT1283B-1A (12-inch), CD1472-D1M (14-inch Mazak CRT), C-5470NS, and the Totoku 12-inch display tube — are increasingly failing as these old-fashioned CRTs age beyond repair. The Mazatrol old system CRTs are becoming impossible to source.

This guide covers the full range of Mazak CRT monitors used on Mazatrol M32, M35, and older Mazatrol control systems, with model-specific specifications and replacement LCD options. We cover 12-inch and 14-inch Mazak CRT to LCD retrofit solutions including the MDT1283B-1A, CD1472-D1M, and the Totoku display tube used in some Mazak machines.

⚠️ Quick Answer: Mazak MDT1283B-1A (12" CRT) and CD1472-D1M (14" CRT) are replaced with industrial TFT-LCD modules. Original connectors, plug-and-play. $355 outright. 18-month warranty.
