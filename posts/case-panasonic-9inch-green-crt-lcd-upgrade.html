Panasonic (Matsushita) 9-Inch Green Phosphor CRT to KTV804 LCD Replacement

The Panasonic (Matsushita) 9-inch green phosphor CRT monitor is a distinctive industrial display known for its characteristic green-tinted screen. These CRTs use P39 phosphor, which produces a green monochrome image preferred in factory environments for reducing operator eye fatigue during extended shifts. Found on various CNC equipment and industrial control panels, these 9-inch green monitors are now aging out — dim displays, flicker, and complete failure are common. The KTV804 TFT LCD is the engineered replacement: a drop-in module using the original signal interface with modern brightness and reliability.

Panasonic (Matsushita) 9-inch green phosphor CRT monitor — front view showing distinctive green screen

The Challenge

The customer operated a Matsushita/Panasonic green CRT on their CNC equipment. After 15+ years, the P39 phosphor had degraded below 30% brightness — the green image was barely visible even in a dim workshop. OEM replacement CRTs were discontinued. A full panel replacement was quoted at over $4,000. The customer needed a practical LCD upgrade matching the original signal and mounting.

Panasonic 9-inch green CRT — top view showing housing

Panasonic 9-inch green CRT monitor — model label

The Solution — KTV804 LCD Module

We supplied a KTV804 9-inch TFT LCD module configured for Panasonic/Matsushita 9-inch CRT replacement. The KTV804 (800x600 resolution, 400 cd/m2, LED backlight) accepts the same RGB video and DC 24V power. The mounting matches the original cutout — no panel modification needed. The upgrade is completely reversible with zero changes to the host equipment.

Installation

CRT removal and LCD installation took approximately 20 minutes. The original mounting holes and bezel opening accepted the KTV804 directly. Original signal cable and DC power connected without any adapter. On power-up, the familiar green-tinted interface appeared on a bright, crisp TFT panel at 400 cd/m2 — more than double the brightness of the aged CRT.
