Frequently Asked Questions

Q: My FANUC 0093 CRT display is blank. What should I do?
A: First check the DC 24V power supply — this is the most common cause of a blank CRT. Measure voltage at the display power input. If voltage is present, the flyback transformer or CRT tube may have failed. Our LCD replacement module bypasses both failure points.

Q: Why is my FANUC 0093 screen flickering?
A: Flickering on a FANUC A61L-0001-0093 CRT is typically caused by a failing flyback transformer, aging capacitors on the deflection board, or loose connector pins. The LCD replacement eliminates all CRT-specific failure modes.

Q: Can I replace the 0093 CRT with an LCD myself?
A: Yes. The LCD module uses the same 20-pin Honda MR-20M connector. Simply unplug the old CRT, plug in the new LCD, and power on. No CNC parameter changes, no rewiring. Installation takes 10-15 minutes.

Q: Is the A61L-0001-0093 the same as D9CM-01A?
A: Yes. The D9CM-01A is the Toshiba OEM part number for the same 9-inch monochrome CRT display. Our LCD module works with both part numbers.

Q: Does the LCD module work with FANUC 0i control?
