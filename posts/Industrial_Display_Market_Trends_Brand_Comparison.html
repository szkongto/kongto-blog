Industrial Display Market Trends & Brand Comparison

1. Market Overview

Market Trends Chart: CRT vs LCD 2015-2026 | Source: Kongto Technology

The global industrial display market was valued at approximately USD 6.2 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach USD 9.1 billion by 2030, growing at a CAGR of 8.1%. Key growth drivers include:

  • Industry 4.0 adoption: Smart factory initiatives worldwide are driving demand for modernized HMI (Human-Machine Interface) displays with touch, higher resolution, and network connectivity
  • CRT end-of-life replacement: An estimated 2.5 million CNC machine tools globally still operate with CRT displays that are increasingly difficult and expensive to repair
  • Harsh environment requirements: Growing need for high-brightness (1,000+ cd/m²), wide-temperature (-20°C to +70°C), and vibration-resistant displays in industrial settings
  • Touch screen HMI expansion: Over 60% of new industrial display installations now include touch capability, up from 25% in 2018

2. Key Market Trends

2.1 CRT-to-LCD Retrofit Acceleration

An estimated 2.5 million CNC machine tools worldwide still use CRT displays. As CRT tubes and components become increasingly scarce, the CRT-to-LCD retrofit market is experiencing rapid growth. Key statistics:

  • CRT tube production has declined by 95% since 2010
  • Average CRT repair cost has increased 40% in the last 5 years due to parts scarcity
  • LCD retrofit adoption rate has grown from 8% in 2020 to 28% in 2025
  • The CRT-to-LCD retrofit market is projected to exceed USD 800 million globally by 2028
  • Asia-Pacific accounts for 45% of retrofit demand, followed by Europe (30%) and North America (20%)

2.2 Touch Screen Integration

Over 60% of new industrial display installations now include touch capability. The trend is driven by:

  • Reduced physical buttons and switches on CNC control panels
  • More intuitive operator interfaces replacing membrane keypads
  • PCAP (projected capacitive) technology enabling multi-touch and glove operation
  • Resistive touch still popular in oily/dusty environments for its lower cost and reliable single-point input

2.3 High-Brightness and Outdoor Displays

Displays with 1,000+ cd/m² brightness are increasingly demanded for outdoor and semi-outdoor industrial applications such as construction machinery, marine systems, and solar panel monitoring. Key trends include:

  • Optical bonding technology reducing glare and improving readability in direct sunlight
  • Automatic brightness control via ambient light sensors becoming standard above 15-inch panels

2.4 Resolution and Size Evolution

Industrial display specifications are converging with commercial display standards:

  • Resolution: XGA (1024x768) remains the most common input resolution in CNC retrofit, but Full HD (1920x1080) is now standard on new machine control panels
  • Screen size: 10.4-inch and 12.1-inch are the most popular CRT replacement sizes; 15-inch and above dominate new installations
  • Aspect ratio: 4:3 still required for legacy CNC signal compatibility; 16:9 growing rapidly for new designs
  • Refresh rate: Typical industrial LCDs run at 60 Hz, with 75 Hz needed for some high-speed CNC applications to avoid flicker under fluorescent shop lighting

6. Future Outlook

The industrial display market will continue to shift toward:

  • Larger displays: 12"-15" becoming standard for new CNC systems, replacing legacy 9" CRT form factors
  • Higher resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD) becoming common in premium systems, with 4K entering inspection and quality-control workstations
  • Wireless display: Wi-Fi 6 and 5G connected displays for remote monitoring and diagnostics, reducing cabling in retrofit installations
  • AI integration: Smart displays with built-in predictive maintenance alerts and anomaly detection, reducing unplanned downtime
  • Sustainability: LED-backlit LCDs reducing energy consumption by 70-80% compared to CRT, with manufacturing and recyclable components
