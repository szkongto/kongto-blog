Quick Diagnosis: 5-Minute Check

  1. Power LED? If off → check PSU fuse/capacitors. Common: RIFA cap failure in MDT1283B PSU.
  2. 15kHz whine? No whine = HV failure (flyback transformer). Most common on 14-inch color CRTs.
  3. Video cable? The 3-row 15-pin connector on CD1472/DR5614 is a known failure point — bent pins or corroded contacts.
  4. Flashlight test? Faint scan lines visible = CRT tube alive but missing video signal → video board fault.
  5. Swap test? Connect known-good LCD module. If it works, your CRT is confirmed dead.

Connector Reference — Mazak Displays (3-row 15-pin)

Mazak display modules use a 3-row 15-pin signal connector. Most models (CD1472, DR5614, Fusion 640) use 10 pins; the MDT1283B uses 6 pins. The exact pin-level mapping varies by production year and control generation, so we do not publish a fixed pinout.
