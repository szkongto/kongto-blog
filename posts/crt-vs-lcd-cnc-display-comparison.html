When to Replace vs When to Upgrade

Choose CRT Repair If:

  • You have a known-good spare CRT available (increasingly rare)
  • Machine is scheduled for retirement within 6 months
  • You need a temporary fix while sourcing an LCD module

Choose LCD Upgrade If:

  • CRT shows any failure symptom (dim, flickering, burn-in, color shift)
  • CRT is over 7 years old
  • You want to eliminate recurring repair costs
  • Operator readability and reduced eye strain matter
  • Machine will be in service more than 12 more months

Safety

CRT monitors contain lethal high-voltage components: the anode operates at 10–15 kV even when powered off (residual charge). CRT repair requires discharge procedures and high-voltage handling expertise. LCD modules operate at 12–24V DC — no high voltage, no shock risk, safe for any technician to install.

Environmental Impact

CRT displays contain leaded glass, phosphors, and vacuum tubes — classified as hazardous electronic waste. LCD modules are , lead-free, and consume 75–80% less electricity. Upgrading to LCD reduces your shop's e-waste footprint and energy costs simultaneously.

Conclusion

The decision is clear for most CNC shops: LCD upgrade provides better image quality, eliminates recurring repair costs, removes high-voltage safety risks, and pays for itself within 12 months.
