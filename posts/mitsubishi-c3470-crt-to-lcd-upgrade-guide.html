Mitsubishi C3470 14-inch CRT to LCD Upgrade Guide

Published: September 11, 2026 | Category: Mitsubishi LCD Retrofit | Author: szkongto01

The Mitsubishi C3470 is a 14-inch colour CRT display module built by Mitsubishi and fitted to Mazak Mazatrol controls. The same physical display also carries the Mazak part number CD1472-D1M, which is why a search for either number lands on the same unit — they are two names for one display, not two products.

That dual numbering is the single most common source of confusion when ordering a replacement. This guide sets out what the C3470 actually is, how it differs from the FANUC-style displays (it runs on AC mains, not DC 24V, and uses a different connector), and exactly what the Kongto LCD module changes.

⚠️ Quick Answer: The Mitsubishi C3470 / Mazak CD1472-D1M 14-inch colour CRT is replaced with a 12.1-inch TFT-LCD industrial panel, 800x600, 400 cd/m2. It keeps the Mazak 3-row 15-pin interface and the original AC 110V-220V supply, so there is no rewiring and no parameter change. $350, outright sale, 2-year warranty.

Three rows separate this upgrade from a FANUC-style retrofit. The C3470 is an AC mains display rated 110V-220V, not a DC 24V unit — so the module keeps the mains input rather than asking for a new power run. The signal is RGB analog over a Mazak 3-row 15-pin connector with 10 pins populated, which is a different interface from the 20-pin Honda used on FANUC displays. And the assembly is heavier, at 3.5 kg, reflecting the larger steel chassis Mazak mounts it in.

Installation Steps

1. Isolate the Cabinet

Switch off the Mazak control and disconnect the machine from mains. This is an AC-powered display, so isolate at the cabinet supply, not at a DC rail.

2. Photograph the Label

Record the C3470 or CD1472-D1M marking and the cable entry before removal. This confirms the listing and gives a reference for reassembly.

3. Remove the CRT

Undo the four mounting screws and withdraw the 14-inch CRT from the bezel. Unplug the Mazak 3-row 15-pin cable and the AC supply leads. No soldering is required.

4. Fit the 12.1-inch Module

Mount the LCD module in the original frame. The screw pattern matches the CRT footprint and the panel sits behind the original bezel opening.

5. Reconnect and Check

Reconnect the 3-row 15-pin cable and the AC supply, then power the control. The picture appears without parameter changes; set brightness on the module.

Allow 10 to 15 minutes. Because the module keeps the original AC 110V-220V input and the original RGB analog signal path, the Mazatrol control is untouched — no parameters, no ladder changes, no re-homing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: My display says C3470, but the manual says CD1472-D1M. Which do I order?
A: Either. They are the same 14-inch colour display under two numbering systems, and both take the same $350 module. Quote the marking printed on the display itself.
Q: Is this the same as a Mazak C5470 or C5470NS?
A: No. C5470 and C5470NS are separate listings with their own module configuration. Use the C5470NS product page if that is what your label reads.
Q: Does the module need a DC 24V supply like FANUC displays?
A: No. The C3470 is an AC mains display rated 110V-220V, and the replacement is compatible with that same AC input.
Q: Will the picture be brighter than the CRT?
A: Yes. Brightness rises from roughly 200 cd/m2 to 400 cd/m2, and resolution from 640x480 to 800x600, so text and graphics are noticeably sharper on the same screen size class.
Q: Can the LCD be fitted without modifying the Mazak cabinet?
A: Yes. The module is built to fit the original C3470 bezel opening using the original mounting screws and the original 3-row 15-pin cable.
