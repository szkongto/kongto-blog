Understanding the EUM-1491A CRT

The EUM-1491A is a 14-inch shadow-mask color CRT running at 640×480 (VGA) resolution with a 15.7 kHz horizontal scan rate. It uses a 9-pin D-Sub (DB-9) connector carrying R, G, B, HSYNC, VSYNC, and DC power. The CRT is heavy (~4-5 kg), generates significant heat, and draws ~30W of power. After years of continuous operation, common failure modes include:

  • Dim image / low brightness — CRT phosphor degradation, typically below 40% after 10+ years
  • Color drift / poor convergence — aging shadow mask and deflection yoke misalignment
  • Screen flicker / jitter — failing flyback transformer or aging electrolytic capacitors
  • Complete no-display — high-voltage section failure or CRT tube exhaustion

Signal Interface — Why It Works

Mitsubishi 14-inch CRTs use a 9-pin D-Sub connector for RGB video. The pinout carries R, G, B, HSYNC, VSYNC, and DC 24V power. Unlike generic monitors that expect VGA analog signals, the Mitsubishi interface uses TTL-level RGB (0-5V logic) with separate composite sync — a signal modern LCDs cannot read without conversion.

The KTV148 is built to decode this exact protocol: it reads the Mitsubishi 9-pin RGB/TTL signal directly, converts it internally to LVDS, and drives the TFT panel at its native 800×600 resolution. No external converter, no video scaler, no software change.

Installation Guide

  1. Power down the CNC and disconnect AC supply. Lock out / tag out.
  2. Remove the old CRT housing (typically 4-6 screws). The CRT weighs ~4-5 kg — use two hands.
  3. Disconnect the DB-9 signal cable and DC 24V power connector from the CRT.
  4. Mount the KTV148 using the included adapter plate. Secure with the provided screws.
  5. Reconnect the DB-9 cable and DC 24V to the KTV148. Connectors are keyed — single orientation.
  6. Power on the CNC. The KTV148 will display the control screen immediately with no parameter changes.
  7. Fine-tune brightness, contrast, and position via the on-screen display (OSD) menu.

Total hands-on time: 15-20 minutes. Difficulty: intermediate — basic hand tools and LOTO required.
