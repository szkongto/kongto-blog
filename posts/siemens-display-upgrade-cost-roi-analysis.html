When Does a New Display Make Sense?

Be honest — there are scenarios where buying new is the right call:

ScenarioNew Display?Reason
Machine is under warranty (display covered)✅ YesFree replacement — no reason to upgrade
Machine is being retired within 12 months✅ Yes (refurb)Don't invest in upgrade for a machine you'll scrap
Display is cracked (not backlight failure)✅ MaybeUpgrade kits usually include new LCD panel — effectively same cost
Display is 3–5 years old, backlight failed❌ NoUpgrade kit is cheaper and will outlast a new CCFL display
Display is 10+ years old, any failure❌ NoOther components (inverter, controller) will fail soon — full upgrade is smarter

What's Included in an LCD Upgrade Kit (Siemens)

Not all upgrade kits are equal. Here's what ours includes — and what to watch for with cheap alternatives:

Our Siemens 6FC3988 / SM0901 Upgrade Kit Includes:

  • New LCD panel (800×600 or 1024×768, depending on model) — industrial-grade, not consumer panel
  • LED backlight array — 50,000+ hour rated life, instant-on, no warm-up
  • Custom interface board — signals compatible with SINUMERIK; no adapter cables needed
  • Mounting brackets — matches original 6FC3988 / SM0901 cutout; drill-free installation
  • 12-month warranty — we stand behind our kits (most cheap kits offer 3–6 months)
Watch out for: Some "upgrade kits" on eBay or AliExpress use consumer-grade LCD panels (meant for monitors, not machine tools). These fail within 12–18 months in oil mist and vibration. Make sure your kit is industrial-grade.
