About the SINUMERIK 840C

The Siemens SINUMERIK 840C was a high-end CNC control introduced in the early 1990s, used primarily on large milling machines, lathes, and transfer lines. It features a modular architecture with separate NCU, PLC, and display components. The 840C's CRT display is a 14-inch or 9-inch monochrome/color unit depending on configuration.

After 25-35 years of operation, these CRTs are failing at an accelerating rate. Siemens has discontinued most 840C display models. Third-party LCD replacement is the practical solution.
