Siemens Sinumerik CRT to LCD Upgrade Guide

Published: June 27, 2026 | Author: szkongto01

Siemens Sinumerik 810, 810D, 820, 820M, and 840D (Power Line) controls are among the most reliable CNC systems ever built — and their aging CRT displays are now failing. The 6FC3988-7FA20, SM0901, 579417TA, and 6FC4600-0AR04 models are widely used across these platforms, including 8.4-inch, 9-inch, and 10.4-inch industrial control CRT form factors.

Critical difference from Fanuc/Mitsubishi: Siemens Sinumerik CRTs use AC 110V or AC 220V power, not DC 24V. Our LCD modules include a built-in AC power supply for direct compatibility. Confirm your machine voltage (110V or 220V) before ordering.

⚠️ Quick Answer: Siemens Sinumerik 810/810D/820/820M/840D CRT displays (6FC3988-7FA20, SM0901, 579417TA) are replaced with industrial TFT-LCD modules. 2-row 16-pin signal connector, built-in AC 110/220V PSU. Plug-and-play, no parameter changes. $399 outright. 2-year warranty.

SINUMERIK System Compatibility Guide

SINUMERIK 808D (entry-level)

The 808D uses a compact color LCD (typically 7" or 10"). These are still in production and replacements are available from Siemens. No upgrade urgency unless the display is physically damaged.

SINUMERIK 828D (mid-range)

The 828D uses a 10.4" color TFT display (part number 6FC5203 series). These are still available but expensive (~$2,000–$2,500 from Siemens). Third-party replacements from Kongto cost significantly less. View replacement →

SINUMERIK 840C (high-end, 1990s)

The 840C was a flagship CNC used on large mills, transfer lines, and heavy machinery. Its CRT displays (9" mono or 14" color) are now 25-35 years old. Siemens has discontinued all 840C display parts.

Good news: The 840C uses the same signal interface as the 810/820/880 family. Our KTV-series modules are confirmed compatible. See our 840C-specific guide →

SINUMERIK 810D / 840D (powerful, older)

Most display failures happen here. These systems from the early 2000s used the 6FC3988 series and SM0901. They are now 15-20 years old and failing regularly. Siemens has discontinued most of these displays.

Upgrade strongly recommended — don't wait for failure. If you have a 810D/840D machine, proactively upgrade. Our factory-direct modules start at $399 with a 2-year warranty.

SINUMERIK 840D sl / 828D sl (newer)

The "sl" (solution line) systems use modern color LCDs with standard interfaces. These displays are still available from Siemens, though third-party options offer better value and faster delivery.

SINUMERIK 850 / 880 (legacy)

The 850 and 880 controls from the 1980s-1990s use the 6FC3988-7FA20 CRT display. These are long discontinued. See replacement →

Product Deep Dives

6FC3988-7FA20 — The Most Common Siemens Display

6FC3988-7FA20 is a 9" monochrome CRT/LCD found on SINUMERIK 810D/840D controls. It is the display that fails most often and the one we sell most replacements for. Common failure: backlight goes dark (CCFL tube burnout) or video board stops outputting image. Our KTV148 replacement is a direct drop-in — same mounting holes, same connector, same power.

6FC3988-7FA20 — SINUMERIK 850/880 Display

The 6FC3988-7FA20 is a variant used on the larger SINUMERIK 850 and 880 controls. Functionally similar to the 6FC3988 but with slight differences in mounting bracket and cable routing. Our replacement module covers both.

6FC5103-0AB01 — 840D/810D Color CRT Upgrade

The 6FC5103-0AB01 is a color CRT used on SINUMERIK 840D/810D controls. Our replacement module includes the correct interface cable for a clean drop-in fit.

SM0901 / 579417 TA — Siemens-Indramat Display

The SM0901 (also labeled 579417 TA) is a 9" display used on SINUMERIK 810D/840D and some Indramat (Bosch Rexroth) controls. Because it cross-brands, getting a replacement from either Siemens or Indramat is slow. Our LCD module replaces it regardless of which CNC control it is connected to.

SM1200 — 840D Power Line Color TFT

The SM1200 (6FC5221-0AA01) is a 12.1" color TFT display used on SINUMERIK 840D Power Line systems. It is a higher-end display compared to the monochrome units. Our KTV121 replacement is a factory-direct alternative at a fraction of the Siemens OEM price.

8.4" Industrial CRT — Compact Siemens Display

The 8.4" industrial CRT was used on SINUMERIK 810/820/840D systems where panel space was limited. Our LCD replacement matches the compact footprint.

Installation

1. Power Down

Turn off Sinumerik system. Unplug AC power. Wait 2 minutes.

2. Remove CRT

Unscrew the CRT housing. Disconnect 2-row 16-pin signal cable and AC power wires.

3. Install LCD

Mount LCD module. Holes align 100% with original CRT positions.

4. Connect

Plug 2-row 16-pin cable into LCD module. Connect AC power. Keyed connectors prevent errors.

5. Test

Power on. LCD displays automatically. Adjust OSD.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Which Siemens SINUMERIK displays do you cover?
A: All common models. See the Cross-Reference table above — we have factory-direct LCD replacements for 6FC3988-7FA20, 6FC5103, 6FC5203, SM0901, SM1200, and 8.4" industrial CRT models. If your model isn't listed, contact us with the part number and we'll confirm.
Q: Is it really plug-and-play? No parameter changes?
A: For the vast majority of installations, yes. Our LCD modules accept the same video signal as the original display. No PLC parameter changes, no software configuration, no adapter cables. Some 840D Power Line systems may need a parameter check — we guide you through it.
Q: What power does the Siemens CRT use — AC or DC?
A: Important: Siemens Sinumerik CRTs use AC 110V or AC 220V power, NOT DC 24V like Fanuc or Mitsubishi. Our LCD modules include a built-in AC power supply compatible with 110-220V. Confirm your machine voltage before ordering.
Q: Can I repair the backlight myself?
A: Technically yes, but it requires opening the display housing, discharging high voltage (inverters can hold a charge), and handling fragile CCFL tubes. Unless you have experience, we recommend sending it to us or replacing the unit. The cost of a new CCFL tube plus your time usually exceeds the cost of an LCD upgrade.
Q: How long does a Siemens display typically last?
A: The display panel itself can last 15+ years, but the backlight (CCFL tubes) typically fails after 8-11 years of normal use. The inverter often fails around the same time. This is why LCD upgrades are so popular — you keep the good panel but replace the failing backlight system with reliable LEDs.
Q: My display is completely dead. Can I ship it to you for diagnosis?
A: Absolutely. We offer a diagnostic service — send us the display, we'll tell you exactly what's wrong and give you a repair or replacement quote. If you proceed with us, the diagnosis is free. Turnaround is typically 2-3 business days.
Q: Do you have replacements in stock, or is there a lead time?
A: We stock LCD upgrade kits for all common Siemens SINUMERIK displays (6FC3988 series, SM0901, and others). Most orders ship within 24 hours. If you need a custom configuration, lead time is typically 5-7 business days.
Q: Can I upgrade the display and keep using my existing SINUMERIK control?
A: Yes — that is the whole point. The LCD module replaces only the display unit. Your SINUMERIK NCU/PLC, drives, motors, and all machine functions remain unchanged. You keep the same control, just with a reliable modern display.
