CGA EGA to VGA Industrial Converter — CNC CRT to LCD Signal Guide

Many older CNC CRTs use IBM-compatible CGA (Color Graphics Adapter) or EGA (Enhanced Graphics Adapter) video standards. These signals are not directly compatible with modern VGA LCD displays. An industrial CGA/EGA-to-VGA converter enables you to use a modern LCD while preserving the original display characteristics.
