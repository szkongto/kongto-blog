Okuma OSP Display Compatibility

Okuma OSP (Okuma Specialized Processor) controls from the 1990s through 2000s used CRT displays that are now reaching end-of-life. Common Okuma CRT models include:

  • OSP5000 / OSP5000L-G — 14-inch color CRT, 9-pin DB-9 interface
  • OSP5020 — 14-inch color CRT, similar to OSP5000
  • OSP7000 — larger format, sometimes 15-inch CRT
  • MDT-1005 — monochrome display used on older OSP models

Our KTV series LCD modules are compatible across this range. The key specification to verify is the signal pinout on your Okuma's display connector. Most Okuma OSP CRTs use a DB-9 connector with TTL-level RGB signals, which our modules decode natively.

Installation Notes Specific to Okuma

Okuma OSP controls often have the CRT integrated into the operator panel rather than a separate monitor housing. This means the LCD replacement mounts inside the existing panel cutout. A mounting adapter plate may be needed if the replacement LCD is smaller than the original CRT (e.g., 12.1" LCD replacing a 14" CRT). The adapter plate is included with our kits and bridges the size gap with a clean, professional finish.
