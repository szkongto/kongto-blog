Planeta (KBA Koenig & Bauer) Printing Press CRT to LCD Replacement — Case Study

Planeta, now part of KBA Koenig & Bauer AG, is one of the world's leading manufacturers of sheetfed offset printing presses. Their press control consoles use industrial CRT monitors for operation, monitoring, and diagnostics. As these CRTs age beyond their 15-20 year design life, failures become common — dim displays, color drift, or complete black screens. OEM CRT replacements are long discontinued. This case study documents a successful custom LCD replacement for a Planeta printing press control console.

Planeta (KBA) sheetfed offset printing press | Source: Kongto Technology

The Challenge

The customer's Planeta press console CRT had become unreadable — the image was severely dim with noticeable color shifting. The CRT was original to the machine (over 18 years old), and replacement CRTs were completely unavailable from KBA. A full console replacement was quoted at a very high cost with extended downtime. The customer needed a practical LCD upgrade that would interface with the existing press control electronics.

Planeta printing press control console with original CRT display | Source: Kongto Technology

Planeta CRT monitor with model and specification label | Source: Kongto Technology

Installation

The LCD replacement was completed on-site in under 30 minutes. The old CRT housing was removed, the custom LCD module was mounted in the existing bezel opening, and the original signal and power cables were reconnected. No modifications to the press control console were required. On power-up, the display was immediately clear and bright — the operator could read all press parameters without strain for the first time in years.
